# MCU sheet — schematic capture spec (prototype rev A)

**Baseline:** 2026-11-17. **Status:** **DRAFT**, written from the hardware guide's MCU and Storage & USB sections. Nothing here has been built. Unlike the power sheet, these circuits do not come from a reviewed FieldMesh board; the gaps the guide left open were filled with the choices below.

| File | Content |
|---|---|
| `mcu-netlist.csv` | Every pin of every part and the net it connects to. Same format as the other sheets; `NC` marks an unconnected pin |
| `mcu-bom.csv` | Grouped BOM with MPNs and LCSC codes for every line except the test pads |
| `../easyeda/capture-mcu.js` | EasyEDA Pro capture script, generated by `python3 hardware/tools/easyeda_capture.py` |

The hardware guide does not draw this sheet yet, and `build_guide.py` does not read these files. Its blocks and headings are in `EXTRA_BLOCKS` in `hardware/tools/easyeda_capture.py` until the guide gets an MCU tab.

**Sources.** GPIO allocation: the guide's *Reserved and allocated GPIOs* table (proposed 2026-10-23, backlog H0-06). Signals from other sheets: the sheet-port tables in `build_guide.py`. microSD and USB-C requirements: the guide's *Storage & USB* section (ADR-012). Module pad numbers and support circuit: Espressif **ESP32-S3-WROOM-1 datasheet v1.8**, Table 3-1 and Figure 9-1 (`references/`): 22 µF + 0.1 µF on 3V3, EN RC delay 10 kΩ / 1 µF, 0 Ω links on USB D−/D+.

**Checked** against these sources: all 41 module pads match Table 3-1; every named GPIO in the guide's table and every sheet port with a GPIO lands on the right pad; `3V3`, `GND` and `VBUS` join the power sheet by name. Nets with a single fitted pin: `AUD_INT_MCU`, `AUD_MCLK` and `MIC1_DC_SENSE` (their parts on the audio sheet are optional) and `USB_VSENSE` (optional divider, not fitted).

Designators run from 401. Every part reuses an LCSC code already in the other BOMs, except U401, J401, J402 (from the guide's parts snapshot) and the 5.1 kΩ CC resistors (C25905, UNI-ROYAL 0402WGF5101TCE).

## Choices made for this sheet (owner approved 2026-11-17)

Marked `Chosen 2026-11-17` in the netlist and BOM notes.

| Item | Guide says | Chosen |
|---|---|---|
| Card supply switch | Load switch, active-high enable on GPIO45, 100 kΩ pull-down; part still to choose | AO3401A P-FET (Q401) driven by a 2N7002 (Q402), both already on the BOM. R415 + C406 give about 1 ms soft start so the card's inrush does not dip 3V3. A load-switch IC with current limit is the alternative |
| USB ESD | ESD protection needed | SRV05-4 (D401), the LTE sheet's SIM TVS: D+, D−, CC1, CC2, with VBUS on its supply pin |
| VBUS sense | Still open (GPIO3 spare) | 15 kΩ / 22 kΩ divider to GPIO3, **not fitted**: 2.8–3.1 V at 4.75–5.25 V |
| Boot and reset | Bring GPIO0 to a pad or button | Test pads for GPIO0, EN and GND (TP401–TP403) rather than buttons |
| Status LED | GPIO43 to GND through a resistor | Green KT-0603G with 2.2 kΩ (about 0.6 mA), as on the power sheet |

## Check on the first capture run

- **J401 USB-C** pad numbers are assumed to be A1…B12. If the EasyEDA symbol numbers them differently, the capture script matches by pin name and lists the mapping. Shell pads are not in the netlist yet.
- **J402 microSD** uses contacts 1–8 from the SD standard. Its card-detect and shell pads are not in the netlist: the capture script lists them as unconnected symbol pins. Add the shell pads to GND; card detect stays unconnected (no GPIO left), or goes to GPIO3 instead of VBUS sense.
