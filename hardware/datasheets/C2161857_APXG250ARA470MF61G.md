## Required external parts and equations

CN3791 input network (CN3791 p.7): electrolytic + 1–10 µF ceramic + 47–100 nF ceramic: C201 + C202 4.7 µF + C203 10 µF + C204 100 nF. OK. Input ripple ≈ ICH × √(D(1−D)) ≤ 0.3 A rms, shared with C203 (lower impedance at 300 kHz). C201 mainly holds the MPPT node steady against panel-cable inductance.

## Layout notes and gotchas

- + terminal close to Q202 source (CN3791 p.9 rule 1); GND to the common power-ground copper with D204 anode and the output capacitors.
