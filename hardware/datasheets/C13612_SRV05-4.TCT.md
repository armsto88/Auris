Pin numbering checked against the "Schematic and PIN Configuration" drawing, SOT-23 6L top view (p. 1): I/O on 1, 3, 4, 6; pin 2 ground; pin 5 the rail. The netlist's names (IO1, REF1, IO2, IO3, REF2, IO4) follow pin order rather than Semtech's I/O numbering, but the capture matches by number, so this is cosmetic.

On the MCU sheet, D401 uses Semtech's option 3 since the 2026-11-17 changes: pin 5 (REF2) is left open and the internal TVS serves as the reference (p. 5). Before the change its pin 5 was on VBUS (review W2).

## Required external parts and equations

None. Positive-pulse clamp ≈ VCC + VF (+ L·di/dt of the trace), negative ≈ −VF (pp. 5–6), so keep the pin-2 ground connection and the pin-5 connection short. Option 2 (100 kΩ in series with pin 5) lowers capacitance further but is unnecessary here (p. 5).

## Layout notes and gotchas

- Place D301 at J301 so the strike reaches the TVS before the 33 pF / 0 Ω network and the module; pin 2 straight to the ground plane through its own via (p. 5).
- Route each SIM line through the TVS pad (pad on the trace, no stub).
- USIM_VDD (pin 5) is a module output that is off when the SIM is idle; the steering diodes then clamp any line to ≈ one diode drop above 0 V, which is harmless because the module drives the lines low with the supply off.

## EasyEDA symbol and footprint

Source: JLCEDA/EasyEDA Official Library ([lceda.cn](https://lceda.cn/), [easyeda.com](https://easyeda.com)), record C13612.

- Symbol: six pins numbered 1–6, named "1"–"6" (no functional names). Number match is all the capture needs; netlist pins 1–6 land on the right pins.
- Footprint `SOT-23-6_L2.9-W1.6-P0.95-LS2.8-BR`: pads 1–3 down one side (0.95 mm pitch), 4–6 back up the other, pad 1 opposite pad 6 — the standard SOT-23-6 order, matching the datasheet top view. Pads 1.1 × 0.6 mm.
