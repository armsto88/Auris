## Required external parts and equations

VMID_PRE = AVDD_PRE × R131/(R130 + R131) ≈ 1.63 V; Thevenin 5 kΩ with 10 µF → 3.2 Hz, τ = 50 ms (README "mid-rail τ = 0.05 s" OK). C128 0.1 µF X7R in parallel handles HF. Using MnO2 tantalum instead of X5R here follows README A5 (no piezoelectric pickup on the bias node); the parallel 0.1 µF X7R's piezo charge is swamped by the 10 µF (≈ 100:1).

## Layout notes and gotchas

- Polarity: anode = pad 1 in this footprint (evidence below). Check the silkscreen "+" on the assembly drawing.

## EasyEDA symbol and footprint

Source: JLCEDA/EasyEDA Official Library ([lceda.cn](https://lceda.cn/), [easyeda.com](https://easyeda.com)), record C7171.

- Symbol: pins numbered **1** and **2**, named "1" and "2" (no "+"/"−" names). Pin 1 ends at x = 30, pin 2 at x = 0; the "+" glyph (strokes at x 21–25, y −5 and x 23, y −7…−3) is drawn on the pin-1 side. So symbol pin 1 = anode.
- Footprint `CAP-SMD_L3.2-W1.6-RD-C7171` (EasyEDA units of 10 mil): pad 1 at (3993.976, 3000), pad 2 at (4006.024, 3000). All polarity marks are on the pad-1 (low-x) side:
  - ComponentPolarityLayer (layer 101) circle at (3992.80, 2996.85);
  - TopSilkLayer (layer 3) "+" glyph: strokes x 3988.19–3992.13 at y 2994.09 and x 3990.16 at y 2992.13–2996.06; a silk bar at x 3998.41 just inside pad 1; silk corner fills at x 3991.90–3993.70;
  - Document layer (12) band at x 3994.88–3995.35 with corner marks at x 3993.70–3994.65.
  
  → **Pad 1 = + (anode), pad 2 = − (cathode).**
- The capture matches netlist rows by number, then name. C127's rows use pin "+" and "−", which match neither a number nor a name on this symbol (the alias list maps "+" only to POS/PLUS/VBAT/BAT+, "−" to NEG/MINUS/GND/BAT−), so both rows would be reported as missing and C127 left unwired.
