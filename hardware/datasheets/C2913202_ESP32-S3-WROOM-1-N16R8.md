## Required external parts and equations

From module DS v1.8 Fig. 9-1 and its notes, HWDG §1.3.2–1.3.13, recomputed for the Auris BOM:

- **3V3 decoupling**: 22 µF (C1) + 0.1 µF (C3) at the 3V3 pad. Auris C401 22 µF X5R 25 V 0805 (C45783) + C402 100 nF X7R 0402. HWDG §1.3.2 asks for ≥ 10 µF on the rail for TX current steps: 22 µF at 3.3 V bias keeps well over 10 µF. OK. The TPS63802 sets no upper limit on output capacitance (SLVSEU9D §10.2.2.3).
- **EN RC**: R = 10 kΩ, C = 1 µF recommended (Fig. 9-1 notes; HWDG §1.3.3). Auris R401/C403 match. t(EN = 0.75 × VDD) = RC × ln 4 = 13.9 ms; TPS63802 reaches regulation ~0.55 ms after its EN (Tdelay 321 µs + Tramp 224 µs typ, SLVSEU9D §8.5), so the chip starts ≈ 14 ms after 3V3 is good. HWDG §1.3.3 adds that an RC alone may not be enough with frequent power on/off or photovoltaic supplies and suggests a ≈ 3.0 V supervisor on CHIP_PU: Auris power-cycles the whole MCU domain every job (F3). Since 2026-11-17 the TPS63802's PG (open drain, released at 95 %, low below 90 % of 3V3 and while disabled) drives ESP_EN with R401 as its pull-up and C403 kept, so the RC delay starts only once 3V3 is good; R229, the old PG pull-up, is removed (review W15).
- **GPIO0**: HWDG §1.3.9 recommends an external pull-up and no large capacitance on GPIO0. Auris now has R417 10 kΩ to 3V3 plus TP401 (F7; only TP401 before the 2026-11-17 changes).
- **USB**: Fig. 9-1 uses 0 Ω links (R4/R6) and unpopulated caps; HWDG §1.3.13 suggests 22/33 Ω series and reserved caps to GND near the chip. R405/R406 0 Ω pads give room for that; caps to GND are not reserved (note).
- **SD (SDMMC)**: HWDG §1.3.12: pull-ups on the SD lines and a series resistor reserved on each trace. Auris: pull-ups on CMD, D0–D3 (to the switched SD_VDD); R418 33 Ω in series with CLK at U401 since 2026-11-17, none on CMD/D0 (F9).
- **GPIO45 strap**: R_eq = 45 kΩ ∥ 100 kΩ = 31 kΩ to GND; Q402 gate is the only other load (no DC path). Low at every reset. Optional hardening: burn EFUSE_VDD_SPI_FORCE = 1 and EFUSE_VDD_SPI_TIEH = 1, which fixes VDD_SPI at 3.3 V and makes GPIO45 a don't-care (chip Table 3-4, last row).
- **BATT_ADC**: VBAT_SW × 100/(220 + 100) = × 0.3125; 3.0 V → 0.94 V, 4.2 V → 1.31 V (82 % of the 1.6 V ATTEN2 range). Thevenin 220 ∥ 100 = 68.75 kΩ; worst input leakage 50 nA × 68.75 kΩ = 3.4 mV at the pin (11 mV at the battery), inside the ±10 mV ATTEN2 error. Sampling charge comes from C213 100 nF; the 2.2 kΩ series resistor and a few-pF sample capacitor settle in nanoseconds; C213 recovers with τ = 68.75 kΩ × 100 nF = 6.9 ms, so read ≥ 35 ms after VBAT_SW rises (always true after boot).
- **SOLAR_ADC**: SOLAR_IN × 150/1150 = × 0.1304; ATTEN3 (2.9 V) → 22.2 V full scale. Thevenin 130 kΩ; leakage error ≤ 6.5 mV (50 mV at the panel). SOLAR_SENSE is live from the panel while the ESP32 is off: through 130 kΩ into an unpowered pin's protection diode the current is a few µA at most (≈ 5 µA at an 8 V cold-panel open circuit; ≈ 16 µA at the 20 V D201 clamp), which cannot power anything.
- **MIC1_DC_SENSE** (if fitted): 1 MΩ source; 50 nA leakage → up to 50 mV error: calibrate or treat as a fault indicator only.
- **Status LED** (R416/LED401): I = (VOH − Vf)/R. Before the 2026-11-17 changes LED401 was KT-0603G, an InGaN emerald green, Vf 3.1 V at 5 mA (JLC C12624). From a 3.31 V pin through 2.2 kΩ, I ≈ (3.31 − 2.6…2.9)/2.2 kΩ ≈ 0.2–0.3 mA (estimate; no LED datasheet read), falling further in the cold. The README's 0.6 mA assumed Vf ≈ 2.0 V (F4). Now LED401 is KT-0805G (C2297, same green InGaN, VF 2.6–3.1 V at 5 mA per JLC) and R416 is 100 Ω: I ≈ (3.3 − 2.6…3.1) V / 100 Ω ≈ 2–7 mA (review M4).

## Layout notes and gotchas

- Antenna end at the board edge with the keep-out of Fig. 3-1 / HWDG §1.4.7; nothing (copper, LTE antenna cable, battery) in it. Keep the LTE antenna (J302 feed) as far from the module antenna as the enclosure allows.
- C401/C402 at pad 2; R401/C403 close to pad 3 with a short EN trace (HWDG §1.3.3 notes interference on CHIP_PU causes resets).
- R418 next to pad 24 (series resistor at the driving end, per the netlist note).
- EPAD: soldering is optional but helps heat; use the recommended paste amount so the castellations still wet (module DS §9 note under Fig. 9-1).
- USB: 90 Ω differential, ground reference under the pair, few vias, ground copper either side (HWDG §1.4.8). Keep the A6/B6 and A7/B7 stubs at J401 short.
- SD: CMD and D0 within ±50 mil of the CLK length (HWDG §1.4.9).
- Production eFuses worth considering: EFUSE_DIS_USB_OTG_DOWNLOAD_MODE (in joint download mode the ROM otherwise drives GPIO38 low, GPIO39 low, GPIO40 high for an external USB PHY, HWDG §1.3.13 Table 12; harmless here because U302 is Hi-Z with the modem off); VDD_SPI force to 3.3 V (above). Never burn EFUSE_STRAP_JTAG_SEL (GPIO3 carries the card detect).
- After any reset GPIO10 (PWR_HOLD) is an input and R224 turns the board off unless VBUS, an uncleared RTC alarm or SW201 holds it (F12).
