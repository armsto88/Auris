## Required external parts and equations

- C_IN at IN, C_OUT at OUT, both close to the pins (§7.4.1). Auris: C101 10 µF, C102 10 µF, 0805 25 V X5R (CL21A106KAYNNNE). TI's own rule of thumb for MLCCs is up to −50 % from DC bias and temperature (§7.1.1), which still leaves ≥ 5 µF against the 0.47 µF effective minimum. The real CL21A106KAYNNNE DC-bias curve was not read.
- No noise-bypass capacitor is needed; the reference has an internal ≈ 0.1 Hz RC filter (§6.3.1).
- EN: no external pull-down is needed for a cold start (internal 500 kΩ, §6.3.2).
- Dropout at Auris load: V_DO ≈ 0.48 Ω × 31 mA ≈ 15 mV (max R_DS(on) from Eq. 1).
- Load-step from the preamp switch: C125 (≈ 11–22 µF effective) charged through R129 10 Ω starts at ≈ 3.3 V / 10 Ω ≈ 0.33 A, τ ≈ 10 Ω × 15 µF ≈ 0.15 ms. This stays under I_CL(min) 360 mA but dips AVDD_A for a fraction of a millisecond while the PCM1863 is running. The README turns the preamp on before the 1 s settling wait, which covers it.

## Layout notes and gotchas

- C101/C102 directly at pins 1/5 with a short GND return to pin 2 (§7.4.1). README layout rule 6 (AVDD_A star from C102) is consistent with this.
- **Released-high EN.** The 500 kΩ pull-down is disconnected while EN > V_EN(HI) (Pin Functions, p. 4). If the ESP32-S3 resets while AUDIO_EN is high, GPIO7 goes to its reset state with no pull (ESP32-S3 datasheet v2.2, Table 2-1), and nothing guarantees EN falls except pin leakage. Firmware should drive AUDIO_EN low early in boot (this also gives the PCM1863, which has no reset pin, a clean power cycle). A 1 MΩ external pull-down would make it deterministic; optional.
- **Reverse current.** If VBAT_SW collapses while the LDO is on, the AVDD_A capacitance can feed back into IN. On this board VBAT_SW carries a 470 µF bulk capacitor (power sheet C219), so it falls slowly and the 27–31 mA audio load keeps the net current forward. Keep the firmware rule "AUDIO_EN low before the main switch Q210 opens". No hardware change needed.
- Dropout-mode recovery can overshoot briefly when VBAT_SW rises back above V_OUT + V_DO (§6.4.3). Not a concern while recording is gated above ≈ 3.35 V.
