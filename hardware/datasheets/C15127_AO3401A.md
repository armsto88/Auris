## Required external parts and equations

**Q210 — main high-side switch (power).** Gate: R228 47 kΩ to RAW_BAT (source; 100 kΩ before the 2026-11-17 changes), C218 47 nF gate–source, pulled low through SW202 + R227 100 Ω by Q207–Q209 (2N7002) or directly by SW201.
- On: VGS ≈ −RAW_BAT (R228 current 64–89 µA at 3.0–4.2 V through a 2N7002 of ≤ 7 Ω — negligible drop) → −3.0 V at an empty cell. OK.
- Off: VGS = 0 through R228. Leakage margin: Q210 starts to conduct at |VGS| ≈ 0.5 V (min |VGS(th)|, lower when hot); leakage into WAKE_N above ≈ 3–5 µA (three 2N7002 drains, SW202, PCB moisture) would turn it partly on. 2N7002 IDSS ≤ 80 nA each at 60 V (JSCJ) → ≤ 11 mV drop on R228 47 kΩ at 25 °C (24 mV with the earlier 100 kΩ): OK. Do not raise R228.
- **C218 does not soft-start the switch.** It sits gate–source, so it only delays the gate: turn-on via SW201/R227: τ = 100 Ω × 47 nF = 4.7 µs; via a 2N7002 in weak enhancement (mA range) ≈ 0.1 ms; once VGS passes the threshold the channel enhances fully within microseconds because nothing limits dVDS/dt (only a gate–drain capacitance plus a series gate resistor would). Turn-off: R228 × C218 = 47 kΩ × 47 nF = 2.2 ms; Q210 opens ≈ τ·ln(VBAT/|Vth|) ≈ 3.3 ms after the wake source releases (4.7 ms and ≈ 7 ms with 100 kΩ before the 2026-11-17 changes). Useful side effect: at battery insertion C218 holds VGS ≈ 0, so the Cgd coupling cannot pulse Q210 on.
- **Inrush into VBAT_SW** at turn-on: C219 470 µF electrolytic (ESR not specified; tan δ 0.20 at 120 Hz → ≈ 0.56 Ω at 120 Hz, lower at 100 kHz, ×≈6 at −40 °C per RVT impedance ratio) + C220/C222 22 µF + C228 22 µF (10 µF before the 2026-11-17 changes) + C221/C223/C229 + audio C101 10 µF ≈ 33 µF ceramic effective (estimated with the 10 µF C228). Path resistance (cells, pack FETs, J203, F202, Q205, Q210) not measured; estimate 0.15–0.4 Ω. Ceramic spike ≈ 4.2 V / 0.15–0.4 Ω = 10–28 A, τ ≈ 5–13 µs; electrolytic branch ≈ 4.2 V / (0.35–0.9 Ω) = 5–12 A, τ ≈ 0.15–0.4 ms. Within IDM −27 A for a single pulse, but it may trip a fast short-circuit detector in the pack's protection IC (type unknown).
- A real soft start would need a gate–drain capacitor and a resistor in series with the whole gate pull-down (e.g. 10 kΩ between SW202 pin 2 and SYS_GATE, 220 nF SYS_GATE→VBAT_SW: dV/dt ≈ (VBAT − 1.4 V)/10 kΩ / 220 nF ≈ 1 V/ms → ≈ 0.5 A into 520 µF), keeping C218 gate–source ≥ 10× the gate–drain cap so battery insertion still cannot turn Q210 on. Measure first.

**Q205 — battery path (power).** Drain BAT_FUSED (pack via F202), source RAW_BAT (charger/system), gate R214 100 kΩ to GND; R215 1 MΩ (to RAW_BAT) is not fitted since 2026-11-17 (review P6).
- Normal: body diode conducts pack → RAW_BAT; gate at GND → VGS = −RAW_BAT = −3.0 V at 3.0 V (with R215 fitted, before the 2026-11-17 changes: gate at 0.091 × RAW_BAT, VGS = −2.73 V). Charging current (S → D) needs the channel: OK.
- Reversed pack, panel dark: RAW_BAT ≈ 0 → VGS ≈ 0, body diode reverse-biased → blocks. OK.
- Reversed pack, panel live: the charger output turns Q205 on, RAW_BAT is dragged down until VGS ≈ VGS(th) → Q205 sits in linear mode with ≈ 4–5 V across it at up to the 600 mA charge current (≈ 2–3 W in a SOT-23) — overheats. Known, accepted in README (keyed connector).
- **R215 served no function** (now not fitted). With R214 alone the gate sits at GND, VGS = −RAW_BAT (better enhancement), the reverse-pack behaviour is identical (gate at GND either way when RAW_BAT ≈ 0), and the 1.1 MΩ divider's ≈ 3.8 µA permanent drain disappears (R214 alone carries only gate leakage, ≤ 100 nA). |VGS| ≤ 4.2 V ≪ 12 V, so no divider is needed for gate protection.

**Q101 — preamp supply switch (audio).** Source AVDD_A (3.3 V LDO), gate R126 100 kΩ to AVDD_A, pulled to GND by Q102 (2N7002 since 2026-11-17; AO3400A before). VGS = −3.3 V → RDS(on) ≈ 50–70 mΩ; load a few mA (OPA1662 + bias) → negligible drop. Turn-on is hard (no gate RC); inrush into C125 22 µF is limited by R129 10 Ω to ≤ 330 mA, τ = 220 µs, drawn from the 300 mA TPS7A2033 — expect a short AVDD_A dip when the preamp is enabled (audio sheet: enable the preamp before recording starts).

**Q401 — SD card supply switch (mcu).** Source 3V3, gate R414 100 kΩ to 3V3, C406 100 nF gate–source, R415 10 kΩ to Q402 (2N7002) drain. On: gate = 3.3 × 10 k/110 k = 0.30 V → VGS = −3.0 V, ≤ 85 mΩ → ≤ 17 mV at 200 mA. OK.
- C406 is gate–source like C218: the gate falls with τ = (10 k ∥ 100 k) × 100 nF = 0.91 ms and crosses |Vth| (0.5–1.3 V) after ≈ 0.15–0.5 ms at ≈ 2–3 V/ms. Because the transfer curve is steep (gFS 17 S), the card's capacitance charges within tens of µs once the threshold is passed — a partial soft start, not the ≈ 1 ms ramp the mcu README described before the 2026-11-17 changes (it now gives ≈ 0.1–0.3 ms). For a defined ramp, put the cap gate–drain (SD_PSW_G to SD_VDD), e.g. 47 nF with R415 10 kΩ: ≈ 5 V/ms → ≈ 0.7 ms ramp, ≈ 50 mA into C404 10 µF.

## Layout notes and gotchas

- Q210/Q205 carry the whole board current: short, wide copper from J203/F202 through Q205, Q210 to C219; drain pad copper as heatsink.
- Keep the SYS_GATE / WAKE_N / RTC_WAKE nodes compact and coated: high-impedance nodes in an outdoor enclosure.
