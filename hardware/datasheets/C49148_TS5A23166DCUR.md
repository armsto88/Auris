U105 (DNP) has the same rows with HPF2_S1/HPF2_S2 and AIN4M_C, and C141 at pin 8. The DCU pin numbering matches the SN74LVC2G66DCU (NO = A, COM = B, IN = C), so the netlist only changed pin names. The Pin Functions table's column says "TSSOP NO." but the drawing beside it is the DCU (VSSOP) package: a datasheet label slip, the numbers are the DCU ones.

Function table (§8.4 Table 1, p.18): IN low = off, IN high = on; both directions conduct. FILT_SEL0 → IN1 → R144 → ≈ 100 Hz corner; FILT_SEL1 → IN2 → R145 → ≈ 1 kHz corner. Both low at power-up (R111/R112) = 36 Hz wide setting. Agrees with the audio README table.

## Required external parts and equations

- **Bypass:** 0.1 µF at V+ (§10, p.20). C140 0.1 µF at pin 8 (C141 for U105). OK.
- **Control inputs must not float** (§6.6 note 3): R111/R112 100 kΩ hold IN2/IN1 low before the PCM GPIOs are configured as outputs (`0x10 = 0x00`, `0x12 = 0x44`). OK.
- **Corner with the shunt across the coupling-cap pair:** f_c = 1 / (2π · 0.11 µF · (40 kΩ ∥ (R + ron))), C122/C123 0.22 µF C0G ±5 % in series = 0.11 µF, 40 kΩ = the PCM1863's 2 × 20 kΩ differential input (tolerance not specified by TI).

| Setting | Shunt (BOM) | 40 kΩ ∥ (R + ron) | f_c, ron = 0 | f_c, ron = 1.8 Ω |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FILT_SEL1/0 = 0/0 | none | 40 kΩ | 36 Hz | 36 Hz |
| 0/1 (default) | R144 22 kΩ | 14.19 kΩ | 101.9 Hz | 101.9 Hz |
| 1/0 | R145 1.5 kΩ | 1.447 kΩ | 1000.7 Hz | 999.6 Hz |
| 1/1 | both | 1.358 kΩ | 1067 Hz | 1066 Hz |

The switch contributes ≤ 0.12 % at 1 kHz; the 5 % capacitor tolerance and the unspecified ADC input impedance dominate (README: measure the corners).

## Layout notes and gotchas

- Place U104 between C122/C123 and the ADC with R144/R145 short; HPF1_S1/HPF1_S2 are high-impedance stubs when open. Keep the switch on the cold side (COM on AIN3M_C).
- C140 at pin 8, return to the analog ground near pin 4.
- The EasyEDA footprint's rows run along X (pin 1 bottom-left, counter-clockwise from the top); C181970's footprint was rotated 90° to that. If U104 was already placed on a PCB with the old footprint, re-check its orientation after the swap.

## EasyEDA symbol and footprint

Source: JLCEDA/EasyEDA Official Library ([lceda.cn](https://lceda.cn/), [easyeda.com](https://easyeda.com)), record C49148.

- Symbol pins 1 = NO1, 2 = COM1, 3 = IN2, 4 = GND, 5 = NO2, 6 = COM2, 7 = IN1, 8 = V+: same names and numbers as the datasheet (p.3) and the netlist rows U104/U105. The capture matches by number.
- Footprint `VSON8_L2.0-W2.3-P0.50-LS3.1-BL`: despite the "VSON" name it has **no exposed pad**; it is a two-row gull-wing pattern with 3D model `US-8_L2.0-W2.3-H0.9-LS3.1-P0.50`. Pads 1–4 in one row, 5–8 in the other with pad 8 opposite pad 1 (counter-clockwise). Pads 0.25 × 0.70 mm, row centres 3.10 mm apart (TI land pattern: 0.30 × 0.85 mm, (3.1) mm, DCU0008A example board layout), pitch 2.0 EasyEDA units = 0.508 mm instead of 0.50 mm: the outer pads sit 12 µm off the lead centre. Acceptable; slightly smaller pads than TI's example.
