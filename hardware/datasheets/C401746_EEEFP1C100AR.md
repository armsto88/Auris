## Required external parts and equations

- CN3791 output network (CN3791 Rev 1.0 p.7): a 10 µF electrolytic plus a 1–10 µF ceramic, low ESR. C207 + C208 4.7 µF + C209 10 µF + C210 100 nF: OK. The datasheet allows ceramic-only output, but warns about DC-bias loss raising BAT voltage with no battery.
- **Keep C207 even though ceramics would do for the charger.** RAW_BAT is hot-plugged through J203 and a pack lead (≈ 0.5–1 µH). With ≈ 10 µF of ceramic alone the plug-in step can ring towards 2 × VBAT (≈ 8 V). That exceeds the CN3791 BAT/CSP (6.5 V) and TPL720 VIN (6.5 V) abs max. C207 (10 µF with 0.85 Ω ESR) acts as an RC damper across that LC: characteristic impedance √(L/C) ≈ 0.25–0.35 Ω, ringing near 50 kHz. F202 adds series damping: 0.04–0.2 Ω with the 1812L110PR this was written for, only 0.020–0.070 Ω with the 1812L200THPR fitted since 2026-11-17, so C207's ESR now does nearly all of it. The size of the remaining overshoot is an estimate. Worth measuring: scope RAW_BAT while plugging the pack (now power README bring-up test 5).

## Layout notes and gotchas
