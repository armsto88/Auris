## Required external parts and equations

- None. TVS facts for the modem-rail decision (owned by the LTE review): the SMAJ5.0A does not conduct until ≥ 6.4 V (min VBR at 10 mA) and clamps at up to 9.2 V. A slow overvoltage from a failed TPS63020 regulation loop therefore passes almost unclamped up to ≈ 6.4–7 V.

## Layout notes and gotchas

- Place at the modem end of VMODEM with a short GND via.
