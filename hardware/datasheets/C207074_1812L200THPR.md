**Hold margin against the ≈ 1 A modem burst (p.2 table):**

| Ambient | 20 °C | 50 °C | 60 °C | 85 °C |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Ihold 1812L200TH | 2.00 A | 1.67 A | 1.50 A | 1.29 A |
| Margin over 1.0 A | 100 % | 67 % | 50 % | 29 % |
| Old 1812L110 (for comparison) | 1.10 A | 0.87 A | 0.80 A | 0.60 A |

Ihold is a steady-state figure; LTE bursts are duty-cycled (ms–s), so the real margin is larger. At 85 °C with ≈ 1.1 A (MCU + modem, low cell) the margin shrinks to ≈ 17 %; F202's own I²R (≤ 85 mW) adds a few kelvin.

## Required external parts and equations

- **Trip current vs the JST PH 2 A rating:** Itrip 3.5 A at 20 °C. Littelfuse gives no Itrip derating; scaling with the Ihold ratio (estimate) gives ≈ 4.5 A at −40 °C and ≈ 2.3 A at 85 °C. Between 2 A and 3.5–4.5 A the PH contacts (2 A with AWG 24, ePH p.1) can be overloaded without F202 tripping. The pack's protection IC covers that window (README "Battery fuse margin" item); F202 is the backstop for hard shorts (≤ 2 s at 8 A, Imax 100 A).
- **Inrush at Q210 turn-on:** estimated 5–12 A for 0.2–0.4 ms (AO3401A file). Far below the I²t that trips F202 (8 A for up to 2 s). No nuisance trip.
- **Voltage drop budget:** 1 A × 0.020–0.070 Ω = 20–70 mV (3× lower than the 1812L110's 0.21 Ω), in series with J203 (≤ 2 × 10 mΩ), Q205 and Q210 (≈ 0.1 V each at a 3.0 V cell).

## Layout notes and gotchas

- PPTC hold current follows the local temperature: keep F202 away from U207/L203 (modem converter), Q210 and L201.
- Littelfuse's recommended pad layout (p.5) is two 1.78 × 3.15 mm pads with a 3.45 mm gap (the 1812L110's EasyEDA footprint `FUSE-SMD_L4.7-W3.4` follows it exactly). This part's EasyEDA footprint `F1812` is smaller (below). Both land on the bottom terminations (D = 0.3–1.2 mm, p.5); connect F202 to wide copper on both sides, which the battery path needs anyway. Smaller copper means a slightly warmer PPTC and a slightly lower effective hold current than the table.
- Maximum paste thickness 0.25 mm, reflow peak 260 °C (p.3).
- Bring-up test 9 records F202's drop and temperature at +50 °C.
