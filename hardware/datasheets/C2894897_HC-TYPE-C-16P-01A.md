## Required external parts and equations

- **CC pull-downs**: a UFP (sink-only) port needs its own Rd = 5.1 kΩ to GND on each CC pin (USB Type-C spec, not in the repo). R403/R404 5.1 kΩ 1 % (C25905), one per pin: correct. Resulting CC voltage with a source's Rp (5 V via 56 kΩ / 22 kΩ / 10 kΩ for default / 1.5 A / 3 A): 5 × 5.1/(Rp + 5.1) = 0.42 / 0.94 / 1.69 V. A source never puts VCONN on a pin terminated with Rd, so CC stays below ≈ 2 V.
- **CC on the SRV05-4**: fine. CC never exceeds the 5 V stand-off (SRV05-4 rev 08/11/04 p. 2). With pin 5 on VBUS (before the 2026-11-17 changes) the steering diode to VBUS drew at most (1.69 − ≈0.4) V / 147 kΩ ≈ 9 µA before VBUS was applied, negligible against Rp; with pin 5 open (fix U1, now applied) it draws nothing. Do **not** move pin 5 to 3V3: the board's 3V3 is off while asleep, and a dead rail behind the steering diode would clamp CC to ≈ 0.5 V, which a 3 A source can read as "no sink" and never switch VBUS on (estimate).
- **USB_WAKE divider (power sheet)**: R225 10 kΩ / R226 100 kΩ since 2026-11-17 (review P5): VBUS × 100/(10 + 100) = 4.55 V at 5.0 V, 4.0 V at 4.4 V, well above the 2N7002's 2.5 V maximum threshold (CJ 2N7002, J Sep 2016, p. 2). Load 110 kΩ (45 µA). Before the change R225 was 47 kΩ: 3.40 V at 5.0 V, 2.99 V at 4.4 V, load 147 kΩ (34 µA).
- **VBUS bulk**: none needed. VBUS feeds only the 110 kΩ divider (R225/R226) and D402, so there is no load to hold up and no inrush. With no capacitance, plug-in ringing can overshoot, which is what the ESD clamp is for: before the 2026-11-17 changes the SRV05-4's internal TVS (pin 5 to pin 2, VRWM 5 V, VBR ≥ 6 V); now (fix U1) D402, a TPD1E10B06 (VRWM 5.5 V, 12 pF, IEC 61000-4-2 ±30 kV contact; TI SLLSEB1G §1, §3 and §5.6; §3 names the USB VBUS pin as a use). A 100 nF ceramic at J401 is optional (USB 2.0 limits device VBUS capacitance to 10 µF; spec not in the repo).
- **D+/D− series**: R405/R406 0 Ω (module Fig. 9-1 R4/R6). See the ESP32 part file.

## Layout notes and gotchas

- D401 between J401 and everything else, with pin 2 straight into the ground plane (SRV05-4 p. 5). D402 at the VBUS pads, also with a short ground return.
- Join A6/B6 and A7/B7 right at the receptacle with the shortest possible stubs; route D+/D− as a 90 Ω pair (ESP32-S3 HWDG §1.4.8).
- Shell legs: front slots 0.60 × 1.70 mm (copper 2.10 mm), rear slots 0.60 × 1.40 mm (copper 1.80 mm) in the drawing. On a 1.6 mm board the 0.90 mm legs end inside the hole: use pin-in-paste and check the pull-out strength of the first boards (U4).
- The shell is the first thing an ESD discharge reaches when a cable approaches: give it a short, wide path to the ground plane (U3).
