## Required external parts and equations

C_L = C111·C112 / (C111 + C112) + C_stray. With 15 pF + 15 pF: C_L = 7.5 pF + C_stray, so the design assumes C_stray ≈ 4.5 pF (XI/XO pin capacitance, not specified by TI, plus traces).

| C_stray | C_L with 15 pF | Cap value for C_L = 12 pF (= 2 × (12 − C_stray)) |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 3 pF | 10.5 pF | 18 pF |
| 4 pF | 11.5 pF | 16 pF |
| 5 pF | 12.5 pF | 14 pF |

If the real stray is only ≈ 3 pF the crystal sees ≈ 1.5 pF less than its rated load and runs fast; with a typical 3225 pulling sensitivity of roughly 10–15 ppm/pF (estimate: YXC gives no motional capacitance) that is ≈ +15–25 ppm. 15 pF is a reasonable first value (TI's PCM1860 typical application also shows 15 pF, SLAS831D Fig. 64). Measure LRCK against a reference (already a README G2 test) and move to 18 pF if it is more than ≈ +10 ppm fast.

## Layout notes and gotchas

- YXC's suggested land (1.4 × 1.2 mm pads, 0.8 mm and 0.5 mm gaps) matches the EasyEDA footprint (see below).
- Tight loop to PCM pins 9/10, ground guard, no signals under the crystal (README rule 5); pads 2/4 to the ground plane.
- Start-up margin is unspecified on both sides: check oscillation start at −40 °C on the prototype (and, if possible, with a series resistor of ≈ 3 × ESR to confirm margin).
