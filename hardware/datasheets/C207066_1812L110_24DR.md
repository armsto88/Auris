## Required external parts and equations

None. Note what F201 can and cannot do: it trips only above 1.95 A (20 °C), more than a small 6 V panel's short-circuit current. It protects against large panels or a bench supply, not against a shorted D201 with a small panel (then the panel is simply short-circuited, which is harmless).

## Layout notes and gotchas

- At the connectors, before D201 (netlist note). Keep it away from L201/D203/D204 heat.
