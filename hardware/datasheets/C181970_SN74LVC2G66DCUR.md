## Required external parts and equations

- Corner with shunt R across the coupling-cap pair: f_c = 1 / (2π · 0.11 µF · (40 kΩ ∥ (R + r_on))). R144 22.6 kΩ → 14.4 kΩ → 100 Hz; R145 1.5 kΩ → 1.45 kΩ → 1.0 kHz; both → 1.06 kHz. Matches the README. The 40 kΩ is the PCM1863's 2 × 20 kΩ differential input (SLAS831D §7.5), whose tolerance TI does not give.
- Decoupling: C140 0.1 µF at pin 8. Unused control inputs must not float (§6.3 note 1): R111/R112 hold them low before the PCM GPIOs are configured.

## Layout notes and gotchas

- Place U104 between the coupling caps and the ADC, with R144/R145 short; HPF1_S1/HPF1_S2 are high-impedance stubs when the switches are open.
- The switch is on the cold side (1B/2B on AIN3M_C), which keeps r_on modulation small. Keep it that way if the network is redrawn.
