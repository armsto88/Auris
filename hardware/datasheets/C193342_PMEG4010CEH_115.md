## Required external parts and equations

- CN3791 requirement (CN3791 Rev 1.0 p.8): both diodes Schottky, current rating ≥ ICH (0.6 A) and VR ≥ maximum input: met.
- Average currents: D203 ≈ ICH × D, D204 ≈ ICH × (1 − D), D ≈ VBAT/VCC ≈ 0.45–0.75 → ≤ 0.4 A each; P ≈ 0.42 V × 0.4 A ≈ 0.17 W.
- Night-time reverse bias (CN3791 p.9 items 2 and 3): D204 sees VBAT (cathode at CHG_SW = RAW_BAT through R202/L201); D203 sees VBAT minus the few hundred mV that SOLAR_IN floats at (its leakage feeds the SOLAR_IN loads through Q202's body diode). Sleep drain from both: ≈ 1.6–2 µA (25 °C), ≈ 25 µA (60 °C), ≈ 180 µA (85 °C), typical values.
- Thermal runaway: the datasheet warns that reverse power can matter for Schottkys (Table 7 note 1). Worst case while switching: VR ≈ 8 V × IR (≈ 1 mA at Tj ≈ 110 °C) × duty ≈ 5 mW: negligible against 0.17 W forward loss. Charging stops above +42 °C battery temperature, which bounds the ambient.

## Layout notes and gotchas

- Give each cathode tab ≈ 1 cm² of copper (Table 7 note 3: 150 K/W instead of 330 K/W).
- Keep the C203–Q202–D203–D204 loop tiny (CN3791 p.9).
