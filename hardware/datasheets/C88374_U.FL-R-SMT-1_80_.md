## Required external parts and equations

None at the connector. Quectel recommends exactly this Hirose U.FL-R-SMT family (EG800K-LA HD V1.0.0 §5.4, p. 46) and a π network (series 0 Ω, two shunt pads not fitted) placed close to the antenna/connector (§5.1.4, Fig. 25): R310 0 Ω with C314/C315 DNP at J302 — matches. Quectel's antenna requirements (Table 26, p. 46): VSWR ≤ 2, efficiency > 30 %, cable loss < 1 dB below 1 GHz, < 1.5 dB at 1–2.3 GHz, < 2 dB above 2.3 GHz.

## Layout notes and gotchas

- Recommended pattern (p. 3): two GND pads 1.05 × 2.2 mm with 1.9 mm between them (4.0 mm overall), SIG pad 1.0 × 1.05 mm; **no conductive traces in the marked area under the receptacle**.
- 50 Ω CPWG/microstrip from module pin 35 through R310 to the SIG pad; GND pads flooded and stitched; via fence at ≥ 2 × trace width from the line (EG800K-LA HD §5.2).
- Clearance under the signal pin (EG800K-LA HD §5.2 last bullet).

## EasyEDA symbol and footprint

Source: JLCEDA/EasyEDA Official Library ([lceda.cn](https://lceda.cn/), [easyeda.com](https://easyeda.com)), record C88374.

- Symbol: pin 1 SIG, pin 2 GND, pin 3 GND — same numbers and names as the netlist.
- Footprint `RF-SMD_FRF05002-JSS103M` (named after a compatible clone): pad 1 1.0 × 1.05 mm on the centre line; pads 2 and 3 1.05 × 2.2 mm at ±1.475 mm (1.9 mm gap, 4.0 mm overall). Pad sizes and spacing agree with Hirose's recommended pattern (p. 3); the SIG-to-GND offset along the other axis (1.52 mm between pad centres) was not dimensioned against the drawing — overlay before layout.
