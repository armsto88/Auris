## Required external parts and equations

- **Output voltage** (Eq. 3 p.15): R1 = R2 × (VOUT/0.5 − 1). TI asks for R2 "in the range of 200 kΩ" (180 kΩ in Table 3). Auris R232 680 kΩ / R233 100 kΩ: VOUT = 0.5 × (1 + 6.8) = **3.900 V**. VFB 495–505 mV in PWM (PS/SYNC high, §6.5) ±1 %, divider ±1.74 % → ±2.7 % worst case, 3.79–4.01 V; EG800K VBAT 3.4–4.3 V. R233 is half TI's suggested value: divider current 5 µA instead of ≈ 2.7 µA; no stated minimum, so acceptable.
  - Option (cost): R232 = 1 MΩ (C26083, basic) and R233 = 150 kΩ (C25755, preferred) → 3.83 V, R2 closer to TI's 180–200 kΩ, removes the extended R232. Costs 70 mV of modem headroom; owner choice.
- **Feed-forward C227 4.7 pF across R232**: not in TI's standard circuit. TI's 2 A example (Fig 28 p.21) uses 4.7 pF **in series with 68 kΩ** across R1 (1 MΩ/180 kΩ). Auris: zero ≈ 1/(2π·680 k·4.7 p) = 50 kHz, pole ≈ 390 kHz, HF gain → 1 (no series R). With ≈ 600 µF on the output the loop crossover is far below 50 kHz, so C227 probably does nothing measurable. Keep as fitted from Hub v2 (bench-tested) or drop it; check a 0.1→0.7 A load step with and without it in bring-up test 9.
- **Inductor peak current** (Eq. 1–2 p.14), VO 3.9 V, f 2.2 MHz (min), L 1.2 µH (−20 %), η 0.9:
  - VI 3.0 V, IOUT 1 A: D = 0.231 → 1.44 + 0.13 = **1.58 A** (+20 % → 1.9 A).
  - VI 3.0 V, IOUT 2 A: 3.02 A (+20 % → 3.6 A).
  - VI 2.5 V, IOUT 1 A: 1.90 A.
  - Start-up into the large output capacitance runs at the average current limit: up to 4.5 A + ≈ 0.07 A ripple/2 ≈ **4.6 A** peak. L203 PCR0420-1R5-M Isat 5.5 A typ (−30 % L): OK (TI-listed parts: 5.1–5.4 A, Table 2 p.15).
- **Input capacitors**: 10 µF X5R/X7R at VIN–PGND recommended; TI's application circuit uses **2 × 10 µF** at VIN plus **100 nF at VINA** (Fig 7 p.13, Table 4 p.16); the EVM uses 2 × 22 µF + 2.2 µF + 150 µF at VIN and 0.1 µF at VINA (SLVU365 Fig 9, Table 2). Auris: C228 10 µF (0603 10 V, ≈ 5 µF effective at 4 V) + C229 100 nF "at VIN". VINA (pin 1) and VIN (pins 10/11) are on opposite sides of the package, so a cap at VIN does not bypass VINA.
- **Output capacitors**: 3 × 22 µF ceramic close to VOUT/PGND; a large cap far away needs small ceramics at the IC (§8.2.2.3). C230–C232 3 × 22 µF + C234 100 nF at U207, C233 470 µF placed at the modem: matches.
- **PG**: TI's circuits use a 1 MΩ pull-up (Fig 7 p.13 R3; EVM R3 1.00 MΩ). VOL is specified as ≤ 0.4 V (0.04 V typ) only at 10 µA sink. With R237 = 10 kΩ to 3.3 V the sink current is 330 µA, 33 × the specified condition. If the PG transistor is resistive (≈ 4 kΩ typ, up to 40 kΩ by the max), the "low" level would be ≈ 0.9 V typ — above the ESP32-S3 VIL max 0.825 V (0.25 × VDD, ESP32-S3 datasheet v2.2 DC table). Firmware waits for MODEM_PG before PWRKEY (LTE README sequence step 1), so a PG that never reads low defeats that check.
- **PG meaning** (§7.3.4 p.10): PG goes low when the average inductor current limit is reached; it is an overload flag, not a voltage-window monitor. During start-up into ≈ 600 µF it is low while the converter charges the output at current limit, then high. PG state with EN low is not specified: firmware should read it only after MODEM_EN is high.
- **EN / R234**: EN is a CMOS input (≤ 0.1 µA). There is no capacitor on EN and the TPS63020 has no external soft-start control; its soft start is internal (§7.4.1 p.10). R234 100 Ω therefore provides no soft start, whatever the BOM note says.
- **PS/SYNC high via R236 100 kΩ**: ≤ 0.1 µA input current → negligible drop; forced PWM keeps fixed 2.4 MHz ripple (good for the radio) at the cost of light-load efficiency while the modem idles with the rail on.
- **Start-up surge into VMODEM** (§7.4.1): average current limit starts at ≈ 400 mA, reaches its nominal 3.5–4.5 A once VOUT ≈ 1.2 V, no timer. With C ≈ 600 µF effective: 0→1.2 V ≈ 1 ms at a ramping limit; 1.2→3.9 V at ≈ 4 A takes ≈ 600 µF × 2.7 V / 4 A ≈ **0.4 ms**, energy ½·C·(3.9² − 1.2²) ≈ 4.1 mJ. Input current at the end of the ramp ≈ 3.9 V × 4 A / (0.9 × VIN) ≈ **4 A** from VBAT_SW. Through an estimated 0.25–0.4 Ω battery path (cells, pack FETs, J203, F202, Q205, Q210 — not measured) VBAT_SW sags by up to ≈ 1–1.5 V for that ≈ 0.4 ms, until the TPS63020's own dynamic current limit (VIN < 2.3 V) throttles it. TPS63802 rides through (UVLO falling 1.2–1.29 V); the audio LDO (TPS7A2033, 3.3 V out) drops out during the dip. Without C233 the surge is ≈ 4× shorter.

## Layout notes and gotchas

- §10.1 p.23: input cap, output caps and inductor tight to the IC; GND (pin 2) and PGND (exposed pad) as separate nodes joined at one point near pin 2; FB divider (R232/R233/C227) next to pin 3 and returned to pin 2.
- 100 nF at VINA pin 1 to GND pin 2 (TI C3 / EVM C8).
- Solder the exposed pad with thermal vias (§10.3 p.24).
- C233 sits at the modem: keep C230–C232 at U207 so the loop sees ceramics first (§8.2.2.3).
