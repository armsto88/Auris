## Required external parts and equations

C301 is the "about 100 µF, ESR ≤ 0.7 Ω" bulk element of Quectel's VBAT array (LA-HD §3.4.4; Ref sheet 5: C0501 100 µF polarised plus 100 nF, 33 pF, 10 pF, 3.9 pF, 1.8 pF). Auris fits C302–C306 with exactly those MLCC values. Droop from C301's ESR alone for a 1 A step: ΔV = 1 A × 0.07 Ω = 70 mV, inside the 0.5 V headroom between 3.9 V and the module's 3.4 V minimum.

## Layout notes and gotchas

- Place at VBAT pins 42/43 with the MLCCs (smallest value nearest the pins); VBAT trace ≥ 2 mm (LA-HD §3.4.4).
- Land pattern: the EasyEDA lands are 1.2 mm (along the axis) × 1.4 mm on 2.7 mm centres, i.e. 3.9 mm overall with a 1.5 mm inner gap. KEMET's IPC-7351 lands for case A (p. 30) are 4.06–6.02 mm overall (V1) with a 0.62–0.98 mm gap (S) for density levels C–A. The EasyEDA pattern is shorter at the toe and wider in the middle than KEMET's least-material pattern; it is a common LCSC footprint and reflows fine, but KEMET level B (1.23 × 1.80 mm lands, 0.82 mm gap, 4.92 mm overall) would give a stronger joint on a vibrating field board.
- Polarity stripe on the body = anode (+) (pp. 33–34).

## EasyEDA symbol and footprint

Source: JLCEDA/EasyEDA Official Library ([lceda.cn](https://lceda.cn/), [easyeda.com](https://easyeda.com)), record C561794.

- Symbol: two pins, number 1 (left, straight plate, "+" mark drawn beside it) and number 2 (right, curved plate); names "1" and "2".
- Footprint `CAP-SMD_L3.2-W1.6`: pads 1 and 2, 1.2 × 1.4 mm, centres 2.7 mm apart; silkscreen polarity bar on the pad-1 side; documentation layer "+" at pad 1 and "−" at pad 2.
- Netlist: pin 1 = VBAT_M (+), pin 2 = GND. **Agrees** with symbol, footprint marks and KEMET's polarity convention (stripe = anode).
