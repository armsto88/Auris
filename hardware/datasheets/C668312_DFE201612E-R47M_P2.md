## Required external parts and equations

See `C2845237_TPS63802DLAR.md`: IPEAK = IOUT/(η(1−D)) + VI·D/(2fL) → 1.85 A at VI 2.5 V, IOUT 1 A; 3.3 A at 2 A. Both below 5.5 A.

## Layout notes and gotchas

- Place directly across U206 pins 9 and 7; short, wide copper; keep FB trace (pin 4) away from it (SLVSEU9D §12.1).
