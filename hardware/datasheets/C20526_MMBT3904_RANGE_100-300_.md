## Required external parts and equations

- **Q203** (cut-out, on = temperature OK): base from TEMP_FAULT_N via R211 47 kΩ, R212 100 kΩ to GND, TEMP_FAULT_N pulled up by R210 10 kΩ to 3.3 V. IB = (3.3 − 0.65)/(10 k + 47 k) − 0.65/100 k ≈ 40 µA. IC = (SOLAR_IN − VCE(sat))/R213 100 kΩ ≤ 0.19 mA at 19 V → forced β ≤ 5: saturated. When TEMP_FAULT_N is low (≤ 0.1 V), VBE ≈ 0.1 × 100/147 ≈ 0.07 V: off.
- **Q204** (clamp, on = stop charging): base fed directly from SOLAR_IN through R213 100 kΩ (no separate base resistor needed: R213 is both Q203's collector load and Q204's base resistor). IB ≈ (6 − 0.65)/100 k = 53 µA at 6 V; collector load = MPPT divider, SOLAR_IN/R204 ≈ 15 µA + CN3791 MPPT pin ±100 nA (CN3791 Rev 1.0 p.4) → saturated, MPPT ≈ 20–50 mV ≪ 1.205 V. Off-state leakage into the MPPT node (Thevenin 80 kΩ): ≤ 50 nA × 80 kΩ = 4 mV (0.3 % of 1.205 V) at 25 °C; charging stops above +42 °C anyway, so the warm-case shift stays small.
- **Q206** (RTC INT inverter, 1 MΩ base path R219 + R222, 1 MΩ collector R223): INT released → IB = (3.3 − 0.5)/2 MΩ ≈ 1.4 µA, IC(sat) = 3.3 µA → forced β 2.4: saturated; RTC_WAKE ≈ tens of mV (Q207 VGS(th) ≥ 1.0 V: off). INT low → Q206 off → RTC_WAKE = 3.3 V − (ICEX + 2N7002 IGSS) × 1 MΩ; at 25 °C ≤ 50 nA + 80 nA → ≥ 3.17 V. Idle cost (no alarm, Q206 on): 1.4 + 3.3 ≈ 4.7 µA — the "latch idle ≈ 5 µA" line in the power README.
- **Q301** (PWRKEY open collector): IB = (3.3 − 0.7)/4.7 kΩ − 0.7/47 kΩ ≈ 0.54 mA; collector pulls PWRKEY, which the module holds at VBAT internally (Vnom = VBAT, VIL max 0.5 V, ≤ 10 nF on the pin; Quectel EG810M-EU HD V1.0 Table 6 p. 19, §3.5.1 Fig. 7 p. 30, §3.7 note 2 p. 34 — U301 since 2026-11-18; the EG800K-LA HD V1.0.0 pin table and §3.5–3.6 notes say the same). Saturated VCE ≪ 0.5 V: OK. GPIO21 has no internal pull at reset (ESP32-S3 datasheet v2.2 pin table), so R303 47 kΩ keeps Q301 off while the ESP32 boots: OK. Load on the collector: C316 10 nF at the module pin (added 2026-11-17, Quectel EG800K-LA Fig. 9 = EG810M-EU Fig. 7) plus Cobo ≤ 4 pF, i.e. Quectel's 10 nF value; Q301 discharges it in well under a microsecond at these currents (estimate). Before the change only Cobo ≤ 4 pF loaded the pin.

## Layout notes and gotchas

- Q206/R219/R222/R223 and Q207's gate are 1 MΩ nodes: keep them small, away from switching nodes, and conformal-coat. Surface leakage of a few hundred nA (condensation) would upset them.
- Q204's collector on MPPT: route MPPT short (it is a 1.2 V regulation node of the charger).
