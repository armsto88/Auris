## Required external parts and equations

- **Wake latch**: any of Q207–Q209 on pulls WAKE_N → R227 100 Ω → SW202 → SYS_GATE low, turning Q210 on. Sink current = RAW_BAT/R228 = 64–89 µA with R228 47 kΩ (42 µA with 100 kΩ before the 2026-11-17 changes) plus discharge of C218 47 nF. With VGS 3.3 V and threshold ≤ 2.5 V the FET sinks milliamps: OK.
- **Q207**: gate from the Q206 inverter through R223 1 MΩ; rise time ≈ 1 MΩ × 50 pF ≈ 50 µs. Default off (Q206 on, RTC_WAKE ≈ 0).
- **Q208**: PWR_HOLD (GPIO10), R224 100 kΩ pull-down; GPIO10 has a 60 µs low glitch at power-up and no pull at reset (ESP32-S3 datasheet v2.2) — harmless (low = release).
- **Q209**: before the 2026-11-17 changes R225 47 kΩ / R226 100 kΩ from VBUS gave 3.23 V at VBUS 4.75 V, 3.0 V at 4.4 V: the weakest drive of the four, and the divider is not needed for gate protection (VGS max ±20 V). R225 is now 10 kΩ (C25744, basic; review P5): 4.3 V at 4.75 V VBUS, 4.5 V at 5 V, and the gate still discharges through R226 when VBUS goes away; it also replaced an extended part (R225 FRC0402F4702TS C2906872).
- **Q402**: SD_PWR_EN (GPIO45: weak pull-down at reset + R402 100 kΩ → card off by default). On: pulls R415 to GND → Q401 VGS = −3.0 V.
- **Q102** (audio, since 2026-11-17, replacing AO3400A): gate from PREAMP_EN (GPIO6) through R127 1 kΩ; R128 100 kΩ holds it off while GPIO6 has no pull at reset. On, it pulls PRE_GATE (R126 100 kΩ to AVDD_A) to GND and turns Q101 on: 33 µA load, 3.3 V drive against Vth ≤ 2.5 V. Off: IDSS ≤ 80 nA into R126 → ≤ 8 mV on PRE_GATE, so Q101 stays off.

## Layout notes and gotchas

- WAKE_N and RTC_WAKE are high-impedance in sleep (100 kΩ and 1 MΩ): compact layout, conformal coat.

## EasyEDA symbol and footprint

Symbol pins 1=G 2=S 3=D; footprint pads 1, 2, 3 standard SOT-23. Matches JSCJ (1 gate, 2 source, 3 drain, p.1) and the netlist rows for Q207–Q209, Q402 and Q102.
