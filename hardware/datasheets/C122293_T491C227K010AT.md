## Required external parts and equations

R116 470 Ω + C114 220 µF: f = 1/(2π · 470 Ω · 220 µF) = 1.5 Hz; τ = 0.10 s (README OK). At 100 Hz the filter attenuates the LDO's ≈ 0.15 µV/√Hz noise (TPS7A20 Fig. 5-65) by ≈ 65×; at 1 kHz by ≈ 650×. MnO2 rather than polymer follows README A5 (polymer leakage limit is higher; KEMET's T491 is 0.01CV).

## Layout notes and gotchas

- Anode (pad 1) to MIC1_BIASF. In this footprint pad 1 is on the **+x side** (mirrored compared with the C7171/C16133 footprints) — check the placement drawing.
- Mic 2 (C136, DNP) uses the same part and pin order.
