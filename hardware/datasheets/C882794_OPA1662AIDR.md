## Required external parts and equations

- Gain (A): 1 + R122/R123 = 1 + 3.0 k/1.0 k = 4 (+12.0 dB). DC gain 1 through C121: f = 1/(2π·1 kΩ·100 µF) = 1.6 Hz.
- Input high-pass: C119 0.22 µF into R121 100 kΩ → 7.2 Hz.
- HF roll-off: C120 100 pF ∥ R122 3 kΩ → 531 kHz.
- Mid-rail: R130/R131 10 k/10 k from AVDD_PRE, C127 10 µF + C128 0.1 µF → 1.63 V, τ = 5 kΩ × 10 µF = 50 ms (3.2 Hz).
- Noise budget at +IN A (my estimate from §5.7 typicals; not in the README):

| Source | 1 kHz | 100 Hz |
|---|---:|---:|
| Op-amp e_n | 3.3 nV/√Hz | 5 nV/√Hz |
| R122 ∥ R123 = 750 Ω thermal | 3.5 | 3.5 |
| i_n × \|Z_+IN\| (≈ 2.45 kΩ at 1 kHz, ≈ 7.1 kΩ at 100 Hz) | 2.5 | 14 |
| R121 100 kΩ thermal, divided by \|Z_+IN\|/100 kΩ | 1.0 | 2.9 |
| Total | ≈ 5.5 nV/√Hz | ≈ 15 nV/√Hz |

  A-weighted over 20 Hz–20 kHz this is ≈ 0.65 µV; with the PCM1863 at +20 dB (≈ 3.6 µV ÷ 4 = 0.9 µV) the preamp path totals ≈ 1.1 µV, the same as the README's figure, but the op-amp is not the only term. Mic self-noise (≈ 2.4 µV) still dominates both paths. Lowering Rf/Rg (e.g. 300 Ω/100 Ω) would cut the 3.5 nV term but needs a 1000 µF Cg for the same corner — not worth it.

## Layout notes and gotchas

- C126 at pin 8 with a short return to pin 4.
- Keep PRE1_INP (100 kΩ node) short and guarded; it is the highest-impedance node in the preamp.
- **Direct mode with the preamp off:** PREAMP_EN low leaves V+ ≈ 0, but MIC1_OUT still reaches +IN A through C119. At ADC full scale the mic swing is ≤ 0.15 V peak, below the input ESD-diode turn-on, so it should stay linear. Worth one THD measurement on the direct path at high SPL with PREAMP_EN low.
- Channel B as an idle follower is stable and harmless but costs ≈ 1.4 mA (≈ 5 mW from the battery). A single OPA1661 would save it if the mic-2 option is dropped.

## EasyEDA symbol and footprint

Source: JLCEDA/EasyEDA Official Library ([lceda.cn](https://lceda.cn/), [easyeda.com](https://easyeda.com)), record C882794.

- Multi-part symbol `OPA1662AIDR.1` / `.2`. Part 1: 1 = OUT A, 2 = −IN A, 3 = +IN A, 4 = V−, 8 = V+. Part 2: 5 = +IN B, 6 = −IN B, 7 = OUT B, **8 = V+, and V− with internal pin number 9** (its displayed number text reads "4"). There is no pad 9 on the SOIC-8 footprint.
- The capture script places part 1, then part 2 to find 5/6/7. Part 2's pins 8 and 9 are left unconnected (reported as "symbol pins not in the netlist"). Pad 8 and pad 4 still get their nets from part 1, but EasyEDA may flag pin 9 as having no pad when the PCB is updated.
- Footprint `SOIC-8_L4.9-W3.9-P1.27-LS6.1-BL`: pads 1–4 on one side, 5–8 on the other, pad 8 opposite pad 1. Matches Figure 4-1.
