## Required external parts and equations

Quectel's network sits at the holder (EG810M-EU HD V1.0 §4.3, Figs. 17–18, pp. 38–40, for U301 since 2026-11-18; same values in EG800K-LA HD §4.3, Figs. 19–20 and Ref V1.2 sheet 6): 0 Ω R306–R308, 33 pF C310–C312, 100 nF C313 (< 1 µF), 15 kΩ R309 USIM_DATA pull-up to USIM_VDD, TVS D301 (< 15 pF). All present with the right values. In Quectel's 8-pin figure the detect switch's common is GND and PRESENCE goes to USIM_DET with a 51 kΩ pull-up to VDD_EXT; Auris does not use detection, so CD stays open.

## Layout notes and gotchas

- Footprint pad row (left to right in the drawing's vertical view, p. 2): C3, C7, C2, C6, C1, C5 on a 1.27 mm pitch, then CD 0.95 mm after C5; pads 0.65 mm wide. Route DATA (C7) and CLK (C3) — adjacent in this row — with ground between them where possible (Quectel §4.3 asks to keep DATA and CLK apart and shielded).
- Two ø0.75 mm locating holes (p. 2) — non-plated; keep copper clear.
- Keep RF (ANT_MAIN/J302) and VBAT away from the holder (Quectel §4.3).

## EasyEDA symbol and footprint

Source: JLCEDA/EasyEDA Official Library ([lceda.cn](https://lceda.cn/), [easyeda.com](https://easyeda.com)), record C428450.

- Symbol pins (number = name): C1 = VCC, C2 = RST, C3 = CLK, C5 = GND, C6 = VPP, C7 = I/O, CD = CD, **8 = EH**. The netlist's pin column (C1, C2, C3, C5, C6, C7, CD) matches the symbol's pin numbers exactly, so number-first matching works; the netlist name "DETECT" for CD differs from the symbol's "CD", which does not matter.
- Footprint `SIM-SMD_SNO-1303-C`: contact pads C3 (x −4.83), C7 (−3.56), C2 (−2.29), C6 (−1.02), C1 (0.26), C5 (1.53), CD (2.48) mm — 1.27 mm pitch and 0.95 mm to CD, 0.65 × 1.2 mm, in the drawing's order (p. 2). Four shell pads all numbered **8**, 0.7 × 2.1 mm, 12.99 mm apart in x and 12.0 mm in y (drawing: 13.69 − 0.70 and 12.00). Two non-plated holes for the locating pegs. **Agrees with the SOFNG drawing.**
- Symbol pin 8 (EH, the four cover tabs) had no netlist row before the 2026-11-17 changes; it is now on GND (`J301,8,EH,GND`), and the PCB net check passed for J301.
