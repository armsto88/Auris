## Required external parts and equations

- **Q201 reverse-polarity switch**: drain at the panel, source at SOLAR_IN, gate to GND through R201 22 kΩ. Correct polarity: body diode conducts first, then VGS = −SOLAR_IN turns the channel on. Reversed panel: SOLAR_IN ≈ 0, VGS ≈ 0, body diode reverse-biased → blocks; VDS = −Vpanel ≤ 30 V, and D201 clamps reverse too. OK.
- **D202 (BZT52C8V2, K at SOLAR_IN, A at gate)**: limits VGS to −Vz (7.7–8.7 V, standard C-series tolerance; the luJing PDF table is font-encoded and not readable). Zener current at SOLAR_IN = 19 V: (19 − 8.2)/22 kΩ = 0.49 mA, R201 dissipation 5 mW. Correct orientation. For a 6 V panel (Voc ≈ 7–9 V cold) the Zener barely conducts; with AO3407A (±20 V) it is margin only, with AO3401A (±12 V) it becomes required.
- **Q202 buck switch**: source at SOLAR_IN (= CN3791 VCC), gate at DRV; DRV high = VCC − 60 mV (off), low = VCC − 6.3 V (on) (CN3791 p.4). CN3791 asks for BVDSS above the highest input voltage and lists "3407A" among suitable FETs (p.8). OK.

Conclusion: AO3401A (C15127) is equal or better on VDS, RDS(on), threshold and Crss, adequate on VGS max in both positions given D202 and the CN3791's ≤ 8 V drive clamp, and worse only on gate charge (immaterial at 300 kHz/0.6 A). Swapping both removes the AO3407A extended line (one $3.07 feeder fee per order) and ≈ $0.015/board. Conditions: D202 must stay fitted (with AO3401A it is what keeps VGS within ±12 V for SOLAR_IN up to the 19–20 V TVS clamp), and Q202's switching node should be checked on the bench for gate ringing beyond 8 V (bring-up test 5).

## Layout notes and gotchas

- Q202, D204 and C203 form the hot loop: keep it tiny (CN3791 p.9–10 layout rules).
- D202 and R201 right at Q201's gate.
