## Required external parts and equations

**Charge current** (p.5–6): ICH = 120 mV / RCS = 0.120 / 0.200 = **0.60 A** (0.55–0.65 A over the VCS spread). R202 dissipation I²R = 72 mW (85 mW at 650 mA) in a 1206 sense resistor: OK. Trickle 17.5 % (105 mA) below 66.5 % × VREG = 2.79 V; termination at 16 % (96 mA); recharge at 95.5 % (4.01 V) (p.4, p.6).

**MPPT** (p.6): VMPPT = 1.205 × (1 + R204/R205) = 1.205 × (1 + 402/100) = **6.05 V**. Pin tolerance 1.18–1.23 V gives 5.92–6.17 V; with 1 % resistors about 5.83–6.27 V. Pin current ±100 nA × (402 k ∥ 100 k) = ±8 mV at the pin (±0.7 %). The panel must reach ≈ 6.17 V open-circuit before the charger starts (p.5 start condition).

**Inductor** (p.8, image): ripple ΔIL = VBAT × (1 − VBAT/VCC) / (f × L), to be kept ≤ 0.3 × ICH = **180 mA**, worst at maximum VCC and minimum L; and separately **L > 5 × (VCC − VBAT) µH**.

| Case | L needed by L > 5(VCC − VBAT) | L needed for ΔIL ≤ 180 mA (f = 300 kHz) | 22 µH (earlier BOM): ΔIL | 47 µH (current BOM): ΔIL |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| VCC 6.05 V (MPPT regulating), VBAT 3.0–4.2 V | ≥ 15.3 µH | ≥ 28 µH (worst VBAT ≈ 3.03 V) | 0.23 A | 0.11 A |
| VCC 7.2 V (Voc 25 °C), VBAT 3.0 V | ≥ 21 µH | ≥ 34 µH | 0.28 A | 0.13 A |
| VCC 8.3 V (Voc −20 °C), VBAT 3.0–4.2 V | **≥ 26.5 µH** | **≥ 38 µH** (worst VBAT 4.15 V; 48 µH at fmin 240 kHz) | 0.31 A (0.49 A at 240 kHz with L −20 %) | 0.15 A (0.23 A worst) |
| VCC 8.8 V (−40 °C), VBAT 3.0 V | ≥ 29 µH | ≥ 41 µH | 0.34 A | 0.16 A |

High VCC with full current is realistic: whenever the panel can supply more than the ≈ 0.45 A the charger needs, the MPPT loop is inactive and VCC rises towards Voc (cold, bright spring mornings). A 22 µH part (−20 % → 17.6 µH) only satisfies the L rule while VCC − VBAT ≤ 3.5 V. **Minimum: 33 µH by the L rule, 47 µH to also meet the ripple guideline** (Node v4's value). Peak current ICH(max) + ΔIL/2 = 0.65 + 0.12 = 0.77 A with 47 µH (0.90 A worst with 22 µH): **Isat ≥ 1.0 A, Irms ≥ 0.7 A**. The BOM now fits SWPA6045S470MT (C36414: 47 µH, 1.4 A sat, 200 mΩ): OK, copper loss ≈ 0.07 W at 0.6 A (the earlier SMMS0420-220M: 0.45–0.51 Ω → 0.16–0.18 W).

**Input capacitors** (p.7): RMS ripple rating ≥ ICH/2 = **0.30 A**; build from an electrolytic, a 1–10 µF ceramic and a 47–100 nF ceramic. Fitted: C201 47 µF 25 V polymer (2.8 A rms, 30 mΩ), C202 4.7 µF 25 V 0805, C203 10 µF 25 V 0805, C204 100 nF 50 V. OK.

**Output capacitors** (p.7): low-ESR; 10 µF electrolytic plus 1–10 µF ceramic. Fitted: C207 10 µF 16 V electrolytic, C208 4.7 µF, C209 10 µF, C210 100 nF. OK (ceramic total slightly above 10 µF, harmless).

**P-FET** (p.8): BVDSS above the maximum input (Q202, now AO3401A, 30 V vs 19.9 V clamp: OK). Pd ≈ VBAT/VCC × Rds(on) × ICH² × (1 + 0.005 dT) ≈ 0.69 × 0.06 Ω × 0.36 × 1.3 ≈ 20 mW. Below 20 V input, conduction loss dominates (p.8). Gate: the internal clamp keeps DRV within 8 V of VCC (p.7), inside the AO3401A's ±12 V.

**Diodes** (p.8): both Schottky, current rating ≥ ICH, VR ≥ maximum input. PMEG4010CEH 1 A / 40 V: OK. Average currents ≈ ICH × D (D203) and ICH × (1 − D) (D204), D ≈ 0.45–0.75 → up to ≈ 0.4 A each, ≈ 0.17 W at VF ≈ 0.42 V → Tj rise ≈ 55 K on a standard footprint (330 K/W), ≈ 25 K with 1 cm² cathode copper (150 K/W) (PMEG Rev 02 Table 7). Charging is blocked above +42 °C battery temperature, so ambient while switching stays ≲ 45 °C.

**Sleep-mode battery drain** (p.9): (1) BAT + CSP ≈ 9 µA at 4.2 V; (2) leakage of the blocking diode D1 (D203) to the input side; (3) leakage of D2 (D204) to GND. Without D1 the VCC pin would draw ≈ 21 µA through L and the FET body diode, and every SOLAR_IN load (R213 → Q204 base ≈ 28 µA, the two dividers, U202) would hang on the battery, so D203 stays. Quantified with PMEG4010CEH Rev 02 Table 8 / Fig. 2 (typical, VR ≈ 4 V):

| Term | 25 °C | 60 °C | 85 °C |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| CN3791 BAT + CSP | ≈ 9 µA (≤ 15 µA spec) | not specified | not specified |
| D203 + D204 reverse leakage | ≈ 1.6–2 µA | ≈ 25 µA (interpolated) | ≈ 180 µA |

**Status LEDs** (p.6–7, Table 1): ILED = (VSOLAR − VF − Vpin)/2.2 kΩ. Red (VF ≈ 1.8–2.0 V at 2 mA): 1.8–1.9 mA at 6.05 V, 2.9 mA at 8.3 V. Green (VF 2.6–3.1 V): DONE occurs with little load on the panel, VCC ≈ Voc → 1.9–2.1 mA at 7.2 V, 2.4–2.6 mA at 8.3 V. Sink capability 7–18 mA: OK. Resistor power ≤ 19 mW (0402: 62.5 mW): OK. Panel power used: ≈ 11–20 mW per LED. Both LEDs pulse when no battery is connected (Table 1).

**Temperature cut-out via MPPT** (owner decision P2/ADR-007, checked here): Q204 pulls MPPT to VCE(sat) < 0.1 V (collector current ≤ 8.8 V/402 kΩ ≈ 22 µA, base current ≈ 53 µA). That is inside the pin's −0.3 V limit. The datasheet describes MPPT only as a regulation input and says normal operation needs MPPT > 1.23 V (p.5): with the clamp on before sunrise the CN3791 does not start; if the clamp comes on while charging, the MPPT loop sees the panel "collapsed" and drives the charge current towards zero. Whether DRV stays fully off and what CHRG/DONE show (probably CHRG low, red LED on with no current) is **not specified** → measure in bring-up test 6.

## Layout notes and gotchas

- p.9–10: input capacitor + close to Q202 source; D203, D204 and R202 close to L201; output capacitors close to R202; keep the C201/C203–Q202–D203–D204–L201 loop short.
- p.9–10: return the COM network (R203/C206) and U201 GND separately to system ground; tie C201–C204 GND, D204 anode and C207–C210 GND on one copper area first, then to ground.
- p.10 Fig. 4: CSP and BAT traces Kelvin-connected straight to R202's pads, same layer, close together.
- p.9 Fig. 3: an optional RC snubber across the catch diode (R5/C5) suppresses tens-of-MHz ringing. Leave a DNP footprint from CHG_SW to GND.
- p.7: no battery-presence detection, and do not connect a battery while the charger is running: it may sit in an undefined state or push a large current into the cell briefly. Field procedure: cover or unplug the panel before swapping the pack.
- No charge timer and no power path (README open item).
- D203/D204 cathode tabs: give each ≥ 0.5–1 cm² copper.
