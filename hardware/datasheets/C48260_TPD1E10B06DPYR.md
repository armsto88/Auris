## Required external parts and equations

None. Place at the connector, before FB102 (as drawn: D101 and C113 on MIC1_OUT_C, FB102 between them and MIC1_OUT). Residual ESD current into the PCM1863 inputs is further limited by FB102, the 0.22 µF coupling caps and the 47 Ω input resistors; the PCM1863 analog pins tolerate −1.7 to +5.0 V (SLAS831D §7.1).

D402 sits on VBUS at J401 since 2026-11-17 (review W2): it replaces the clamp VBUS used to get from D401 pin 5, which is now open so the ESP32's D+ pull-up cannot back-feed VBUS.

## Layout notes and gotchas

- Shortest possible ground connection from pin 2 to the ground plane at J101.
- D402: at J401's VBUS pads, with the same short ground return.
- Only MIC1_OUT_C is protected; MIC1_SRC, MIC1_RET and MIC1_SHLD are 0 Ω to GND at the connector, which is adequate.
- **Footprint size:** TI's DPY0002A example land pattern is 2 × 0.3 × 0.5 mm pads at 0.7 mm centre spacing (SLLSEB1G p. 20, Example Board Layout); the package terminals are 0.2–0.3 × 0.45–0.55 mm at 0.65 mm pitch (p. 19, Package Outline). The EasyEDA footprint uses 0.6 × 0.6 mm pads at 1.0 mm centres (a generic 0402 land). The inner gap (0.4 mm) is the same, but each pad overhangs the part end by ≈ 0.3 mm, which encourages skew or tombstoning of a 1.0 × 0.6 mm no-lead part.

## EasyEDA symbol and footprint

Source: JLCEDA/EasyEDA Official Library ([lceda.cn](https://lceda.cn/), [easyeda.com](https://easyeda.com)), record C48260.

- Symbol: pins 1 and 2 (names "1", "2"). Netlist pin numbers 1/2 match (D101/D102 and D402). Symmetric device, so orientation is not critical.
- Footprint `X1SON-2_L1.0-W0.6-P0.65-BI-1`: pad 1 at x 3995.925, pad 2 at x 3999.862 (EasyEDA units of 10 mil) → 1.0 mm centres; both pads 2.362 × 2.362 units = 0.6 × 0.6 mm. The name says P0.65 but the pads are not at 0.65 mm.
