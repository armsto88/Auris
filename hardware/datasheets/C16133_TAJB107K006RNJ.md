## Required external parts and equations

Cg sets the preamp's DC gain to 1: f = 1/(2π · R123 · C121) = 1/(2π · 1 kΩ · 100 µF) = 1.6 Hz (README "Preamp Cg τ = 0.1 s" OK). MnO2 rather than X5R follows README A5 (no piezoelectric pickup in the gain network; at +12 dB any charge here is amplified ×3).

## Layout notes and gotchas

- Anode (pad 1) to PRE1_CG; keep R123–C121 short and its ground return close to the op-amp's pin 4.
