## Required external parts and equations

None. Reverse connection is covered by Q201 (P-FET) and the bidirectional D201.

## Layout notes and gotchas

- Silkscreen "+" and "−" next to each terminal: the EasyEDA footprint has only wire-entry arrows (documentation and silkscreen layers), no polarity text.
- F201 and D201 directly behind the terminals (netlist notes).
- J201 and J202 are in parallel: two panels must have the same Vmp (netlist note).

## EasyEDA symbol and footprint

Source: EasyEDA library record C695629. Symbol pins "1"/"2" (designator prefix "U?", rename to J). Footprint `CONN-TH_DB301V-3.5-2P-GN`: two plated through-hole pads 1 (x ≈ 3986) and 2 (x ≈ 4000), 3.5 mm apart, Ø1.0 mm holes (drawing: 1.00 mm). Netlist pins 1/2 match by number. OK.
