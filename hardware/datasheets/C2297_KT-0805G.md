## Required external parts and equations

Model: VF(I) ≈ VF(5 mA) + Rd · (I − 5 mA), Rd ≈ 45 Ω read from the KT-0603G IF–VF curve between 1.5 and 9 mA (p.5, typical). The VF temperature coefficient is not given by KENTO; ±0.2 V over −40/+85 °C is an InGaN-typical estimate.

**LED401 (status, mcu sheet):** I = (VOH − VF(5 mA) + Rd · 5 mA) / (R416 + Rd), VOH ≈ 3.2–3.3 V from the ESP32-S3 3V3 pin at a few mA.

| Case | VF(5 mA) 2.6 V (bin VM) | VF(5 mA) 3.1 V (bin VQ) |
|---|---:|---:|
| 25 °C | 5.7–6.4 mA | 2.2–2.9 mA |
| −40 °C (VF + 0.2 V, estimate) | 4.3–5.0 mA | 0.9–1.6 mA |
| +85 °C (VF − 0.2 V, estimate) | 7.1–7.8 mA | 3.6–4.3 mA |

The mcu README's "2–7 mA" is right at 25 °C. GPIO43 is U0TXD: the ROM boot log and the idle-high TX line light LED401 briefly on every wake (ESP32 file, finding F14).

**LED202 (charge done, power sheet):** I = (VSOLAR − VF − VDONE) / (R207 + Rd), VDONE ≈ 0.1–0.3 V (CN3791 sinks 7–18 mA at 1 V, Rev 1.0 p.4, so ≤ 0.3 V at 2–3 mA, estimate).

| SOLAR_IN | LED202 current | R207 2.2 kΩ dissipation |
|---|---:|---:|
| 6.05 V (MPPT point) | 1.3–1.6 mA | ≤ 6 mW |
| 7.2 V (Voc 25 °C; DONE usually means little panel load, VCC near Voc) | 1.8–2.1 mA | ≤ 10 mW |
| 8.3 V (Voc −20 °C) | 2.3–2.6 mA | ≤ 15 mW |
| 8.8 V (Voc −40 °C) | 2.5–2.8 mA | ≤ 18 mW (0402 rating 62.5 mW) |
| 19.9 V (D201 clamp, surge only) | ≈ 7.5–7.8 mA | ≈ 0.13 W for the surge duration |

LED202 is powered from the panel only: no night drain (D203 blocks back-feed). These match the CN3791 file's status-LED figures.

## Layout notes and gotchas

- JLC placement: check LED202/LED401 polarity in the placement preview (KENTO's 0805 cathode mark not read; the footprint marks the cathode at pad 2).
- LEDs inside a sealed enclosure need a light pipe or window to be seen; LED202 spends ≈ 8–25 mW of panel power while lit.

## EasyEDA symbol and footprint

Source: JLCEDA/EasyEDA Official Library, record C2297 (symbol name "0805G (Green)"). Symbol pin 1 = **A**, pin 2 = **K**. Footprint `LED0805-R-RD`: pads 1.0 × 1.4 mm, centres 2.10 mm apart; documentation "+" at pad 1 and "−" at pad 2; silkscreen with chamfered corners and an arrow pointing to pad 2 (cathode); marker dot near pad 1. Symbol and footprint agree (pad 1 = anode). The netlist rows use A/K, matched by name, so the numbering (opposite to the KT-0603G record C12624) does not matter. OK.
