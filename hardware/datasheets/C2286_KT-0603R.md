## Required external parts and equations

ILED = (VSOLAR − VF − VCHRG)/R206 = (6.05 − 1.85 − ≈ 0.05)/2.2 kΩ ≈ 1.9 mA at the MPPT point; 2.9 mA at 8.3 V. CN3791 CHRG sinks 7–18 mA (CN3791 p.4), so the resistor sets the current.

## Layout notes and gotchas

- Inside a sealed enclosure the LED is only useful at bring-up/service.
