## Required external parts and equations

Q201 (AO3401A, VGS max ±12 V, `C15127_AO3401A.md`) source at SOLAR_IN, gate pulled to GND by R201 22 kΩ, D202 from gate (A) to source (K). |VGS| = voltage across D202 = min(VSOLAR, VZ at the actual current).

| SOLAR_IN | D202 current (VSOLAR − VZ) / R201 | VGS | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6.05 V (MPPT) | below the knee, ≤ 3 µA leakage | ≈ −6.0 V | Q201 fully on (AO3401A rated at −4.5 V) |
| 8.3 V (Voc −20 °C) | ≤ (8.3 − 7.79) / 22 k = 23 µA | ≈ −7.8 to −8.3 V | OK |
| 8.8 V (Voc −40 °C) | ≤ 46 µA | ≈ −7.8 to −8.6 V | OK |
| 19.9 V (D201 SMF12CA clamp, surge) | (19.9 − 7.79) / 22 k = 0.55 mA | ≤ −8.6 V | OK, ≥ 3.4 V margin to 12 V |

- R201 dissipation at the surge clamp: (19.9 − 7.79)² / 22 kΩ = 6.7 mW (0402: 62.5 mW). OK.
- Reversed panel: SOLAR_IN ≈ 0 V, D202 unbiased, Q201 off; D202 sees no stress.
- Night: SOLAR_IN ≈ 0 V (D203 blocks back-feed), so D202 adds no battery drain.
- A cold Zener has a slightly lower VZ (positive tempco above ≈ 5 V; typical for 8.2 V, not from a datasheet): only lowers |VGS| on a cold panel, harmless.

## Layout notes and gotchas

- Place D202 at Q201's gate and source pins, R201 close by.

## EasyEDA symbol and footprint

Source: JLCEDA/EasyEDA Official Library, record C22395557. Symbol pin 1 = **K**, pin 2 = **A**. Footprint `SOD-123_L2.7-W1.6-LS3.7-RD` (3D `SOD-123_L2.7-W1.6-H1.4-LS3.7`): pads 0.91 × 1.22 mm, centres 3.27 mm apart; pad 1 has the silkscreen cathode bar beside it, thickened outline corners and the component-marker dot, documentation "−" at pad 1 and "+" at pad 2. Symbol, footprint and the usual SOD-123 band-at-cathode agree. The netlist rows use "K"/"A", which match the symbol pin names directly (the BZT52C8V2 record needed the K → C alias). OK.
