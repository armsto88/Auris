## Required external parts and equations

ILED = (VSOLAR − VF − VDONE)/R207: VF 2.6–3.1 V → 1.9–2.1 mA at 7.2 V, 2.4–2.6 mA at 8.3 V. With VF up to 3.1 V the green LED needs VSOLAR ≳ 3.5 V to light at all. Fine, since DONE is only active with the panel lit.

## Layout notes and gotchas

- JLC placement: in this EasyEDA footprint pad 1 is the **cathode**, while KENTO's drawing numbers the anode ①. Check LED202/LED401 polarity in JLC's placement preview (the silkscreen arrow points to the cathode pad).

## EasyEDA symbol and footprint

Source: EasyEDA library record C12624. Symbol: pin 1 = **K**, pin 2 = **A** (the opposite numbering from the KT-0603R record C2286). Footprint `LED0603-RD`: pad 1 at x ≈ 3992.7 with documentation "−", silkscreen triangle pointing to it and chamfered outline corners; pad 2 with "+". Symbol and footprint agree with each other (pad 1 = cathode) and with the part's physical cathode mark. The netlist uses A/K, matched by name, so the numbering difference does not matter. OK.

**Basic-part alternative:** KT-0805G, C2297, JLC **basic**, 3.15 M in stock, 0.016 USD. JLC parameters: 2.6–3.1 V at 5 mA, 430 mcd, λd 513–528 nm, 100 mW, −40 to +85 °C: the same die ratings as the KT-0603G (datasheet not read). EasyEDA record C2297: symbol pin 1 = A, pin 2 = K; footprint `LED0805-R-RD` with "+" at pad 1. Electrically equivalent; the only change is the 0805 footprint.
