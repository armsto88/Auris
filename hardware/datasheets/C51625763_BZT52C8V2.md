## Required external parts and equations

- With R201 = 22 kΩ from gate to GND: IZ = (VSOLAR − VZ)/R201. Normal operation (≤ 8.8 V) → < 0.05 mA (below the knee, gate–source ≈ VSOLAR). Surge at 19.9 V → 0.53 mA, VGS ≈ −8.2 V.
- The Zener is mandatory now that Q201 is the basic AO3401A (VGS ±12 V; BOM change 2026-11-17): the TVS clamp alone (19.9 V) would exceed it. VZ max 8.7 V (+ knee) leaves ≥ 3 V margin.
