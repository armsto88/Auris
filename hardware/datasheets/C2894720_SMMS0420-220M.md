## Required external parts and equations

CN3791 Rev 1.0 p.7–8 (inductor selection): ΔIL = VBAT·(1 − VBAT/VCC)/(f·L), keep ΔIL ≤ 0.3 × ICH, and also L > 5 × (VCC − VBAT) µH; f = 300 kHz typ (240–360 kHz, p.4). ICH = 0.12 V / R202 0.2 Ω = 0.6 A → ΔIL limit **0.18 A**.

| Case | VCC | VBAT | ΔIL (22 µH, 300 kHz) | Worst (17.6 µH, 240 kHz) | 5 × (VCC − VBAT) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| At MPPT set point | 6.05 V | 3.0 V | 0.229 A | 0.358 A | 15 µH |
| Panel with surplus (CV / bright) | 7.5 V | 3.75 V | 0.284 A | 0.44 A | 19–22.5 µH |
| Cold panel near Voc | 8.0 V | 3.0 V | 0.284 A | 0.44 A | 25 µH |

- Nominal ripple already exceeds 0.18 A at the MPPT point; L for ΔIL ≤ 0.18 A at VCC 6.05 V: ≥ 28 µH nominal, ≥ 44 µH allowing −20 % L and 240 kHz → **47 µH**, which is the Node v4 value (the README notes Node v4 used 47 µH, replaced by this Hub v2 part for stock reasons).
- Peak current stays well under Isat (0.6 + 0.44/2 ≈ 0.82 A worst), so the consequence is extra ripple current and core/conduction loss, not saturation.
- Options from the same SXN catalogue (LCSC codes not checked): SMMS0650-470M 47 µH, 300 mΩ max, Isat 2.2 A (p.12); SMMS1040-470M 47 µH, 155 mΩ max, Isat 4.5 A, 10 × 10 mm (p.14); SMMS1040-330M 33 µH, 92 mΩ max (p.14). The 10 × 10 mm 47 µH part cuts the copper loss from ≈ 0.18 W to ≈ 0.06 W.

## Layout notes and gotchas

- CN3791 p.9: D203/D204 close to the inductor, R202 close to the inductor, output capacitor close to R202; Kelvin sense lines from CSP/BAT to R202.
