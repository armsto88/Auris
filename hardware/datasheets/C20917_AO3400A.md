## Required external parts and equations

- R128 100 kΩ holds the gate low while GPIO6 is high-Z (ESP32-S3 GPIO6 has no default pull, and shows a 60 µs low-level glitch at power-up — harmless here, it keeps the preamp off).
- R127 1 kΩ limits GPIO current into Ciss 630 pF; gate rise ≈ 0.6 µs. Fine.
- Off-state: IDSS ≤ 1 µA at 30 V (p.2) into R126 → ≤ 0.1 V on PRE_GATE: Q101 stays off.
