## Required external parts and equations

- **Output voltage** (Eq. 3 p.19): R1 = R2 × (VOUT/VFB − 1), VFB = 500 mV, R2 (FB–GND) must not exceed 100 kΩ (§9.3.6 p.12, §10.2.2.5 p.19). Auris R230 510 kΩ / R231 91 kΩ, both 1 % 0603: VOUT = 0.5 × (1 + 510/91) = **3.302 V**. Before the 2026-11-17 cost pass R230 was 511 kΩ (3.308 V), the exact pair in TI Table 10-5 (p.20). Tolerance: VFB ±1 % (PWM) plus a divider ratio error of up to ±1.7 % (two 1 % parts) → ±2.7 % worst case, 3.21–3.39 V; PFM adds a small positive offset/ripple (Fig 10-12). Inside the ESP32-S3's 3.0–3.6 V. Divider draws 5.5 µA (only while awake).
- **Inductor peak current** (Eq. 1–2 p.18, at the minimum boost input): D = (VO − VI)/VO; IPEAK = IOUT/(η(1−D)) + VI·D/(2·f·L).
  - VI = 2.5 V (VBAT_SW sag), VO = 3.3 V, IOUT = 1 A, η = 0.9, f = 2.1 MHz, L = 0.376 µH (−20 %): D = 0.242 → IPEAK = 1.47 + 0.38 = **1.85 A**; +20 % margin → 2.2 A.
  - Same at IOUT = 2 A: 3.31 A, +20 % → 4.0 A. L202 (DFE201612E-R47M, rated 5.5 A at −30 % L) covers both.
  - In overload the peak limit can reach 5.75 A, a little above 5.5 A; acceptable because TI lists this inductor family (Table 10-2 p.19: DFE201612E 0.47 µH, 5.5 A, 26 mΩ).
- **Input capacitor**: 10 µF X5R/X7R at VIN/GND recommended, can be increased without limit (§10.2.2.4 p.19). C222 22 µF + C223 100 nF at the pins: OK.
- **Output capacitor**: one 22 µF nominal for VO ≤ 3.6 V; DC bias must leave ≥ 7 µF (§10.2.2.3). 2 × 22 µF (C224, C225) + C226 100 nF: OK with margin; the ESP32 module's own capacitance adds to it (no upper limit).
- **PG**: high-Z above 95 % of VOUT, low below 90 % (Table 9-1 p.13). Since the 2026-11-17 changes PG drives ESP_EN: R401 10 kΩ to 3V3 → 330 µA when low, within the 1 mA sink rating (VPG;Low ≤ 0.4 V at 1 mA). Before, R229 100 kΩ to 3V3 → 33 µA; R229 is removed. PG is undefined below the rising UVLO with VO < 1.8 V (Table 9-1); then R401/C403 still hold EN low through their RC. When PG pulls low it also discharges C403 1 µF: a brief peak above the 1 mA condition (≤ 5.4 µJ); the datasheet gives no PG current maximum, so this is a note only.
- **EN**: tied to VBAT_SW. The precise EN threshold (§9.3.2 p.10) could set a 3V3 undervoltage cut-off with a divider; not needed because the battery pack has its own protection.

## Layout notes and gotchas

- §12.1 p.28: input and output capacitors right at the pins with short, wide traces; FB trace away from L1/L2; R230/R231 return to AGND (pin 3); join the AGND node to the power ground near pin 3/pin 8 only.
- DLA0010A land pattern (p.36): pads 0.25 mm wide on 0.5 mm pitch, pins 1–5 0.6 mm long, pins 6/7/9/10 0.9 mm, pin 8 1.3 mm. Small HotRod package: use the TI stencil (pin 8 at 83 % paste).
- MODE = low means PFM at light load: 3V3 ripple bursts at low load. The audio ADC digital supply should not share this rail without filtering (audio sheet uses its own LDO from VBAT_SW: OK).
