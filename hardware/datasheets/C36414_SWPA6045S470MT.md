## Required external parts and equations

CN3791 Rev 1.0 p.8: ripple ΔIL = VBAT · (1 − VBAT/VCC) / (f · L), keep ΔIL ≤ 0.3 × ICH; and L > 5 × (VCC − VBAT) µH. ICH = 120 mV / R202 0.2 Ω = 0.60 A (0.55–0.65 A, p.4) → ripple limit **0.18 A**. f = 300 kHz typ, 240–360 kHz (p.4).

| VCC | VBAT | ΔIL nominal (47 µH, 300 kHz) | ΔIL worst (37.6 µH, 240 kHz) | L rule 5 × (VCC − VBAT) | Peak IL worst (0.65 A + ΔIL/2) |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 6.05 V (MPPT point) | 3.0 V | 107 mA | 168 mA | 15.2 µH | 0.73 A |
| 6.05 V | 3.7 V | 102 mA | 159 mA | 11.7 µH | 0.73 A |
| 6.05 V | 4.2 V | 91 mA | 142 mA | 9.2 µH | 0.72 A |
| 8.3 V (Voc at −20 °C) | 3.0 V | 136 mA | 212 mA | 26.5 µH | 0.76 A |
| 8.3 V | 3.7 V | 145 mA | 227 mA | 23.0 µH | 0.76 A |
| 8.3 V | 4.2 V (≈ VCC/2, worst) | 147 mA | 230 mA | 20.5 µH | 0.77 A |
| 8.8 V (Voc at −40 °C, extrapolated) | 4.2 V | 156 mA | 243 mA | 29.0 µH (at 3.0 V) | 0.77 A |

- **L rule:** satisfied in every case even at −20 % (37.6 µH ≥ 29 µH).
- **Ripple rule:** nominal ripple ≤ 156 mA < 180 mA in every case. With L at −20 % and the oscillator at its 240 kHz minimum, ripple reaches 0.19–0.24 A once VCC rises above ≈ 7 V (panel above its MPP: bright, cold mornings); that costs a little extra ripple current, not saturation. Acceptable; the earlier 22 µH part was 0.23 A at nominal values.
- **Peak current:** ≤ 0.77 A worst against Isat 1.4 A. Nominal 0.60 + 0.147/2 = 0.67 A.
- **Copper loss:** I² × DCR = 72 mW at 0.60 A (60–85 mW over 0.55–0.65 A), ≈ 3 % of the ≈ 2.2 W delivered to a 3.7 V cell; +8 % for a 45 °C winding (copper +0.39 %/K). Compare SWPA5040S470MT 354 mΩ: 127 mW; SMMS0420-220M 450–510 mΩ: 162–184 mW. Core loss not known (PDF not read); small at ΔIL ≈ 0.1–0.15 A.
- **Self-heating (estimate):** if the 1.2 A rating means a ≈ 40 K rise (common for this class, not confirmed), 0.6 A gives ≈ 10 K.

## Layout notes and gotchas

- CN3791 p.9–10: L201 next to D203/D204 and R202; keep the C201/C203–Q202–D203–D204–L201 loop short; CSP/BAT Kelvin lines to R202.
- 6 × 6 mm footprint and 4.5 mm height: larger than the 4.6 × 4.2 × 2.0 mm SMMS0420 it replaces. Check enclosure clearance above L201.
- The winding start is not marked on the footprint. Wire-wound parts with resin shielding leak some field: keep L201 away from the microphone inputs and the preamp (charger runs at 240–360 kHz only in daylight, but magnetic pickup into high-impedance audio nodes is avoidable by distance).
