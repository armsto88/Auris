## Required external parts and equations

- CIN ≥ 1 µF at IN (p.5 pin table). RAW_BAT carries C207 10 µF, C208 4.7 µF, C209 10 µF and C210 100 nF, but at the charger. U204 needs one within a few millimetres, or its own: C236 10 µF (CL10A106KP8NNNC) was added at pin 3 on 2026-11-17.
- COUT ≥ 2.2 µF (p.5), 2.2–10 µF ceramic (p.1): C214 + C215 OK.
- Load budget: DS3231M ICCS ≤ 130 µA max (≈ 88 µA typ) + latch ≈ 5 µA + 3.3 µA through R219 while INT is asserted. Ground current rises only ≈ 0.2 % of load (Fig. 1 p.8).
- KEEP_ALIVE = min(3.3 V ±3 %, VBAT − ≈ 0 V). With the pack at 3.0 V the RTC runs at ≈ 3.0 V, still above the DS3231M VPF (2.45–2.70 V), so it stays on VCC.

## Layout notes and gotchas

- C236 at pin 3, returned to pin 1 (before C236 was added the advice was to put U204 next to C208/C209 on RAW_BAT).
- C214/C215 close to pin 2. C216 sits at U205 pin 2.
- The quiescent figure is typical only. The datasheet gives no maximum, so measure it in bring-up test 1.
