## Required external parts and equations

See `C15483_TPS63020DSJR.md`: IPEAK (Eq. 2, SLVS916I p.14) = 1.58 A at VI 3.0 V/IOUT 1 A; 3.0 A at 2 A; ≈ 4.6 A during the current-limited start-up into ≈ 600 µF. All below 5.5 A typical.

## Layout notes and gotchas

- Tight to U207 L1/L2 pin pairs; both pins of each pair connected on the pad copper.

2-pin symbol (1, 2), footprint `IND-SMD_L4.4-W4.1_PCR0420` with pads 1 and 2; body 4.4 × 4.1 mm matches the drawing (A 4.4, B 4.1, C 2.0 max; p.1). Non-polar.
