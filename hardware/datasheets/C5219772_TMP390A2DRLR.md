## Required external parts and equations

- **Hot trip** (Table 7-1 p.8): 2.49 kΩ → trip **+42 °C**, reset +37 °C (5 °C column) — R208 FRC0402F2491TS 1 %. OK.
- **Cold trip and hysteresis** (Table 7-2 p.9): 18.7 kΩ in the 5 °C-hysteresis column → trip **+5 °C**, reset **+10 °C**, and sets 5 °C hysteresis for channel A too. R209 0402WGF1872TCE (C11476) 1 % since the 2026-11-17 owner decision. OK. Before, R209 was 21.5 kΩ (0402WGF2152TCE) → trip 0 °C, reset +5 °C. (21.5 kΩ in the 10 °C column does not exist — the 10 °C column uses 442 kΩ for 0 °C.)
- Resistors must be E96 1 %, ≤ 100 ppm/°C (§8.2.2.2 p.13); tolerance contributes no trip error within that. Both parts are 1 % thick film: OK.
- **Bypass**: 0.1 µF at VDD (§8.2.1.1 p.11, §9 p.17): C212 100 nF — place at pin 5. C211 10 µF is the HT7533 output cap.
- **Outputs tied together**: allowed (§8.2.1.2 p.12, §8.2.2.1). TEMP_FAULT_N = OUTA AND OUTB: high only when +5 °C < T < 42 °C (with hysteresis; 0 °C before the 2026-11-17 change), low when too cold or too hot.
- **Downstream logic** (checked in all states):

| State | TEMP_FAULT_N | Q203 | TEMP_CLAMP | Q204 | MPPT | Charging |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| +5…42 °C, panel live | ≈ 2.84 V (R210 into R211/R212) | on: IB ≈ 40 µA, IC ≤ (19 V − 0.1)/100 kΩ = 0.19 mA → saturated | ≈ 0.05 V | off | divider R204/R205 | yes |

## Layout notes and gotchas

- The sensor measures the PCB, not the cells; the pack is on a cable (J203). Mount U203 where the board is closest to the pack and away from U201/L201/Q202 and U207.
- Keep SETA/SETB traces short, no test points (≤ 50 pF, low leakage).
- Trip test: driving SETA or SETB high forces the output low (§7.3.2 p.9) — usable once in production test, not as a feature.
