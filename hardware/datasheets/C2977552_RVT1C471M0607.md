## Required external parts and equations

- C219 sits on VBAT_SW with C220/C222/C228 ceramics; C233 on VMODEM with C230–C232 near U207 and C301 (100 µF, 70 mΩ) at the module. TI allows unlimited capacitance on both converter inputs and on the TPS63020 output (SLVS916I §8.2.2.3–8.2.2.4; SLVSEU9D §10.2.2.4).
- Side effects of the bulk: C219 dominates the inrush when Q210 turns on (see `C15127_AO3401A.md`); C233 dominates the ≈ 4 A, ≈ 0.4 ms start-up surge of the modem rail (see `C15483_TPS63020DSJR.md`).

## Layout notes and gotchas

- Polarity: pad 1 = +, pad 2 = − (footprint silk "+" at pad 1, "−" and chamfered base corners per EasyEDA record; symbol "+" at pin 1).
- Keep ceramics between the IC pins and this cap; it does nothing at MHz.

## EasyEDA symbol and footprint

Symbol: 2 pins, "+" drawn at pin 1 (straight plate side). Footprint `CAP-SMD_BD6.3-L6.6-W6.6-LS7.6-RD`: pads 1 (left, "+" silk) and 2 ("−" silk). Netlist C219/C233 pin 1 = + rail, pin 2 = GND: matches.
