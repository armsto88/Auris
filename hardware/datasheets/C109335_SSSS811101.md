## Required external parts and equations

- **Minimum load:** R228 was lowered from 100 kΩ to 47 kΩ for this switch: I = RAW_BAT / (R228 + R227 + RDS(on)) ≈ RAW_BAT / 47.1 kΩ = 64 µA (3.0 V) … 89 µA (4.2 V) whenever a wake source pulls or SW201 is pressed. ≥ 50 µA. In sleep the switch carries nothing (no cost).
- Side effect, already in the netlist note: Q210 turn-off delay R228 × C218 = 47 kΩ × 47 nF = 2.2 ms (was 4.7 ms).
- With SW202 open, SYS_GATE stays at RAW_BAT through R228: no wake source (RTC, PWR_HOLD, USB, SW201) can turn Q210 on.

## Layout notes and gotchas

- Right-angle part: the actuator must reach the board edge or an enclosure opening. 1.5 mm travel.
- Land drawing (p.3): three terminal pads 0.7 mm wide (0.75 mm from pad ① to the first post hole), extending 1.0–2.5 mm from the post centreline; four ground pads 1.0 × 0.8 mm, 6.3 mm apart inside edges, 1.4 mm gap; two Ø0.9 mm holes 3.0 mm apart. The EasyEDA footprint has the holes as NPTH.
- Reflow peak 260 °C ≤ 3 s; hand soldering 330 °C ≤ 3 s (p.4).
- Silkscreen ON / SHIP labels after the first-article meter check.

## EasyEDA symbol and footprint

Source: JLCEDA/EasyEDA Official Library, record C109335. Symbol pins 1, 2, 3 (switch) and 4, 5, 6, 7 (frame), names equal numbers. Footprint `SW-SMD_SSSS811101`: pads 1, 2, 3 at 3.0 mm (1–2) and 1.5 mm (2–3) spacing, 0.70 × 1.50 mm, so pad 2 is the common ② and pad 1 is ①; pads 4–7 are 0.8 × 1.0 mm ground pads at 7.30 mm × 2.20 mm centres (matches the 6.3 + 2 × 0.5 mm and 1.4 + 0.8 mm of the land drawing); two Ø0.9 mm holes 3.0 mm apart. The three terminal pads span 1.25–2.75 mm from the hole centreline, 0.25 mm further out than ALPS's 1.0–2.5 mm; they still cover the terminal (documentation-layer lead outline ends 2.19 mm from the centreline). Netlist rows SW202 1–7 match by number.
