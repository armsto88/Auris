## Required external parts and equations

- FB101 with C108 0.1 µF + C109 10 µF (≈ 5–6 µF effective) forms an LC low-pass for the PCM digital rails. With an assumed 0.5–2 µH low-frequency bead inductance (not given), f0 ≈ 1/(2π√(LC)) ≈ 30–90 kHz and Q ≈ √(L/C)/R_DC ≈ 0.6–1.3 — at most mild peaking, and the PCM1863's 80 dB PSRR (SLAS831D §7.6) covers it.
- FB102 sits in series with the ≈ 2.3 kΩ mic source. At audio frequencies the bead is ≈ its DCR (0.45 Ω), so it adds no audible loss, noise or distortion. Ferrite non-linearity needs flux levels far above what 0.6 mA DC plus µA–mA AC produce; even a 1 Ω impedance modulation against 2.3 kΩ would be < −120 dB. A bead in the µV signal path is therefore sensible here: it isolates the ESD clamp and the cable from the ADC/op-amp inputs at RF.

## Layout notes and gotchas

- FB102 between the connector-side parts (D101, C113 on MIC1_OUT_C) and the circuit side (R117, C116, C119 on MIC1_OUT), as drawn. Keep that order.
- FB101 at the PCM side of the AVDD_A star, with C108/C109/C110 on the DVDD_A side next to pins 13/14.
