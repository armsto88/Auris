## Required external parts and equations

- Basic circuit (p.13): 10 µF at input and 10 µF at output (drawn as polarised capacitors). Auris: output C211 10 µF X5R + C212 100 nF; input from SOLAR_IN's C201–C204.
- The datasheet gives no output-capacitor ESR range. Ceramic-only output is common practice but is not stated as supported; check for oscillation at bring-up (cheap scope check on TEMP_3V3).
- Below VIN ≈ 3.4 V the output follows the input. The TMP390 runs from 1.62 V, the CN3791 cannot run below its 3.1–4.1 V UVLO, and with TEMP_3V3 dead Q203 is off, so R213/Q204 keep MPPT clamped (fail-safe, see TMP390 file).

## Layout notes and gotchas

- The SOT89 tab is VIN (pin 2), not GND: do not tie the tab copper to the ground plane.
- Put C204 (100 nF) or C202 near pin 2 if U202 is placed away from U201.
