## Required external parts and equations

- None for the holder. The DS3231M needs no VBAT capacitor for backup use; only 0.1–1 nF if contact bounce is filtered (19-5312 p.10). C217 was 100 nF and is 1 nF since the 2026-11-17 changes (see the DS3231M file).
- The cell is not in the BOM. JLC fits the holder only; the BR1220 is fitted by hand (power-bom.csv BT201 note).

## Cell (owner decision 2026-11-18)

- **Fitted cell: Panasonic BR1220 (order code BR-1220/BN), rated −30 to +80 °C**, 3 V, 35 mAh, 0.7 g (Panasonic BR1216/BR1220 individual specification, read 2026-11-18 from the Digi-Key-hosted copy https://mm.digikey.com/Volume0/opasdata/d220001/medias/docus/9/BR1216,BR1220.pdf; not in `pdf/`). Same 12.5 × 2.0 mm size code (1220) as a CR1220, so it fits this holder; same 3 V nominal, inside the DS3231M VBAT range of 2.3–5.5 V (19-5312 p.2).
- **No CR1220-size cell is rated to −40 °C.** Renata's coin-cell designer's guide gives −30/+70 °C for the CR1220 MFR; Maxell rates its CR1220 −20 to +85 °C (both as checked for the owner decision; neither document is in `pdf/`). **Correction:** this note earlier said, from memory, that Renata rates its CR coin range −40 to +85 °C. That is wrong for the CR1220: Renata's −40/+85 °C applies to other sizes such as the CR1225 and CR1616.
- **Why not a 12.5 mm × 2.5 mm CR1225-size cell**, which does reach −40 °C (Renata CR1225 −40/+85 °C; Panasonic BR1225A −40 to +125 °C)? It needs a CR1225 holder. The JLC candidates were rejected: Antenk BT-S-G0-A-CR1225B (C52748081) has no datasheet, so its polarity cannot be confirmed; TE BAT-HLD-012-SMT is rated only −30 to +70 °C. So the CR1220-2 holder stays.
- **Bench test at −40 °C:** cold-soak the board with KEEP_ALIVE removed and check that the DS3231M keeps time on the cell (BT201 is the RTC's only source then), and that the holder still contacts the cell.

## Layout notes and gotchas

- Footprint has two non-plated holes for the holder's locating pegs; keep them clear of copper.
- Insert the cell with the main pack already connected and SW202 on (KEEP_ALIVE up). This is the DS3231M's preferred cure for contact bounce (p.10).
- Mark "+" on the silkscreen beside pad 1 so the polarity can be checked visually; the EasyEDA silkscreen layer has none (the "+"/"−" marks are on the documentation layer only).

## EasyEDA symbol and footprint

Source: EasyEDA library record C70381 (`cache/easyeda/C70381.json`).

- Symbol: two pins named "1" (left, x = 0) and "2" (right, x = 40). Battery graphic: plates at x = 15 (long), 18 (short), 21 (long), 24 (short). Pin 1 connects to the long-plate end (positive), pin 2 to the short-plate end (negative).
- Footprint `BAT-SMD_CR1220-2`: pad 1 at x ≈ 3962, pad 2 at x ≈ 4030, both 4.5 × 2.3 mm. Holder outline: a ≈ 14.2 mm circle centred at x ≈ 3996, plus a 3.9 × 14.5 mm frame around the pad-1 end. Documentation layer (12): a "+" (horizontal bar 3955–3969 plus vertical bar 3961–3963) beside pad 1 and a single horizontal bar (4023–4037) beside pad 2.
- Symbol and footprint agree: **pad 1 = +, pad 2 = −**.
- Netlist before the 2026-11-17 changes: pins "+" and "-". The capture matches by number, then by name; the symbol's names are "1"/"2", and the alias list maps "+" only to POS/PLUS/VBAT/BAT+, so **neither BT201 row would have been placed.** The rows now use pin numbers (1 = + RTC_VBAT, 2 = − GND); BT201 was re-captured and wired, and the PCB net check passed.
