**Why this part.** Owner decision 2026-11-18 (review M2/W8, finding F2 of [`C2913202_ESP32-S3-WROOM-1-N16R8.md`](C2913202_ESP32-S3-WROOM-1-N16R8.md)): the N16R8 is rated only −40…+65 °C because of its octal PSRAM, and a sealed enclosure in Australian sun goes above that. The N16R2 is the same module (same footprint, pads, straps and EasyEDA symbol pin map) with 2 MB **quad** SPI PSRAM inside the chip package and the −40…+85 °C rating (module DS Table 1-1 p. 3). Flash is still 16 MB quad SPI. Because quad PSRAM uses the chip's SPI0/1 lines (chip select SPICS1) and not GPIO33–37 (chip DS Table 2-14, p. 31), module pads 28–30 (GPIO35–37) are ordinary GPIOs on this variant; Auris leaves them open as spares.

**Chip inside.** The module DS v1.8 block diagram (§2, Fig. 2-1) names the ESP32-S3R2 for R2 modules. Chip DS v2.2 Table 1-1 (p. 13) lists the ESP32-S3R2 (2 MB quad PSRAM, −40…+85 °C, VDD_SPI 3.3 V) as end of life, replaced by the ESP32-S3RH2 (2 MB quad PSRAM, −40…+105 °C, VDD_SPI 3.3 V, chip revision v0.2 only; note 6 refers to a PCN). Either chip keeps the module at its −40…+85 °C module rating; VDD_SPI is 3.3 V in both.

## Required external parts and equations

Unchanged from the N16R8 (same module schematic, module DS v1.8 Fig. 9-1 and HWDG §1.3.2–1.3.13); recomputed values for the Auris BOM:

- **3V3 decoupling**: 22 µF + 0.1 µF at the 3V3 pad (Fig. 9-1). Auris C401 22 µF X5R 25 V 0805 + C402 100 nF. HWDG §1.3.2 asks for ≥ 10 µF on the rail for TX current steps. OK.
- **EN RC**: R = 10 kΩ, C = 1 µF (Fig. 9-1 notes; HWDG §1.3.3). R401/C403 match; t(EN = 0.75 × VDD) = RC × ln 4 = 13.9 ms, starting when U206 PG releases ESP_EN (≈ 0.55 ms after the TPS63802 is enabled). The supervisor HWDG §1.3.3 asks for is the TPS63802 PG (F3).
- **GPIO0**: external pull-up recommended (HWDG §1.3.9): R417 10 kΩ + TP401.
- **USB**: 0 Ω links R405/R406 as in Fig. 9-1; HWDG §1.3.13 suggests 22/33 Ω and reserved caps to GND (F18).
- **SD (SDMMC)**: pull-ups on CMD, D0–D3 to the switched SD_VDD; R418 33 Ω in series with CLK at U401 (F9). Since 2026-11-18, D403 (SRV05-4, ≤ 5 pF per line) clamps SD_CLK (card side of R418), SD_CMD, SD_D0 and SD_CD at J402, with its rail pin on 3V3 (`C13612_SRV05-4.TCT.md`).
- **GPIO45 strap**: 45 kΩ ∥ 100 kΩ = 31 kΩ to GND; low at every reset. On this module VDD_SPI is already fixed by eFuse (module DS §8 note, p. 39), so GPIO45 is a don't-care for the flash supply.
- **BATT_ADC**: VBAT_SW × 100/(220 + 100) = × 0.3125; 3.0 V → 0.94 V, 4.2 V → 1.31 V. Thevenin 68.75 kΩ; leakage error ≤ 3.4 mV at the pin. Read ≥ 35 ms after VBAT_SW rises (τ = 6.9 ms).
- **SOLAR_ADC**: SOLAR_IN × 150/1150 = × 0.1304; ATTEN3 → 22.2 V full scale; Thevenin 130 kΩ; a few µA at most into the unpowered pin from a live panel.
- **MIC1_DC_SENSE** (if fitted): 1 MΩ source; up to 50 mV leakage error.
- **Status LED**: I = (VOH − Vf)/R ≈ (3.3 − 2.6…3.1) V / 100 Ω ≈ 2–7 mA for KT-0805G.

## Layout notes and gotchas

- Antenna end at the board edge with the keep-out of Fig. 3-1 / HWDG §1.4.7; nothing (copper, LTE antenna cable, battery) in it. Keep the LTE antenna (J302 feed) as far from the module antenna as the enclosure allows; Quectel asks for ≥ 20 dB isolation between antennas (`C20421924_EG810MEULA-I05-SNNSA.md`).
- C401/C402 at pad 2; R401/C403 close to pad 3 with a short EN trace (HWDG §1.3.3).
- R418 next to pad 24; D403 at J402 (card side of R418).
- Pads 28–30 (GPIO35–37) have no net: leave them unconnected copper, no trace stubs.
- EPAD: soldering is optional but helps heat; use the recommended paste amount so the castellations still wet (module DS §9 note under Fig. 9-1).
- USB: 90 Ω differential, ground reference under the pair (HWDG §1.4.8). SD: CMD and D0 within ±50 mil of CLK (HWDG §1.4.9).
- Production eFuses worth considering: EFUSE_DIS_USB_OTG_DOWNLOAD_MODE (F16). Never burn EFUSE_STRAP_JTAG_SEL (GPIO3 carries the card detect).
- After any reset GPIO10 (PWR_HOLD) is an input and R224 turns the board off unless VBUS, an uncleared RTC alarm or SW201 holds it (F12).
