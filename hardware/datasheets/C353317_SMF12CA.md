## Required external parts and equations

- Clamp vs downstream: VC 19.9 V → MPPT pin = 19.9 × 100/502 = 3.96 V (< 6.5 V); Q201 (AO3401A, VGS ±12 V) gate–source limited by D202 (MMSZ5237B since the 2026-11-17 cost pass, 7.79–8.61 V; BZT52C8V2 before) to ≈ 8.2 V; Zener current (19.9 − 8.2)/22 kΩ = 0.53 mA.
- **12 V-nominal panel** (Voc ≈ 21–22 V at 25 °C, ≈ 24–25 V cold): above VBR, so D201 would conduct continuously. The panel's short-circuit current × ≈ 14 V (several watts) against a 1 W steady-state rating would destroy D201. F201 trips only above 1.95 A (20 °C), so a small 12 V panel would not trip it. Raising the TVS (SMF24CA: VBR 26.7 V, VC 38.9 V) would let surges exceed the CN3791's 30 V abs max. Auris therefore supports 6 V-class panels only.

## Layout notes and gotchas

- Short, wide path from PANEL_FUSED and from GND (netlist note); place right after F201 at the connectors.

## EasyEDA symbol and footprint

Source: EasyEDA library record C353317. Symbol: pin 1 named "A2", pin 2 named "A1" (bidirectional). Footprint `SOD-123FL_L2.8-W1.8-LS3.7-BI`: pads 1 and 2. The netlist uses numbers 1/2, so the match is by number. Orientation does not matter for a bidirectional part.
