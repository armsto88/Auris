- Only the 2 A part keeps ≥ 1 A hold to +85 °C. Its trip (3.5 A at 20 °C, 2.0 s at 8 A) is above the JST PH 2 A rating. The pack's own protection IC is the primary short-circuit protection; F202 remains a secondary fuse against a hard downstream fault.

## Layout notes and gotchas

- Keep F202 away from U207/L203 (modem converter) and Q210 heat: PPTC hold current follows local temperature.
- Bring-up test 9 already records F202's drop and temperature at +50 °C.

## EasyEDA symbol and footprint

Source: EasyEDA library record C163516. Symbol pins "1"/"2" (prefix "U?", rename to F). Footprint `FUSE-SMD_L4.7-W3.4`: pads 1 and 2. Netlist pins 1/2 match by number. The 1812L200THPR's EasyEDA record (C207074) uses symbol pins "1"/"2" (prefix F?) and footprint `F1812` (pad centres 3.7 mm apart, against 5.2 mm here). Both are 1812 land patterns, so the netlist rows stay as they are and the footprint follows the part.
